------------------------------------------------------------
What C1 does NOT establish
------------------------------------------------------------

- It does not establish that stage2 output is *correct*.
  (B2's differential established component correctness
  against the C reference oracle at stage0/stage1/stage2;
  the C1 S2 hash happens to equal S0/S1 because the B1
  component is itself deterministic and tiny.)

- It does not establish B3 success.
  Only a stage3 build that *consumes* S2 would do that.

- It does not authorize any production mutation.
