ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP04 — C2 — stage3 symbol binding
=================================================

Per ACT §22: `build/hcc-bootstrap04` MUST contain
`_BootstrapScanIdent` as an external symbol from the
linked S2 B1 object.

------------------------------------------------------------
nm output
------------------------------------------------------------

```text
$ nm build/hcc-bootstrap04 | grep BootstrapScanIdent
000000010007c318 T _BootstrapScanIdent
```

Stage3 has exactly one definition of `_BootstrapScanIdent`.

------------------------------------------------------------
Comparison with stage2
------------------------------------------------------------

```text
$ nm build/hcc-bootstrap03 | grep BootstrapScanIdent
000000010007c318 T _BootstrapScanIdent
```

Stage2 and stage3 share the same `_BootstrapScanIdent`
address (000000010007c318). This is the expected outcome
of deterministic link order: the B1 component is identical
across stages and is placed in the same `.text` slot.

------------------------------------------------------------
Required output
------------------------------------------------------------

```text
STAGE3_CONTAINS_B1_SYMBOL = YES  (_BootstrapScanIdent T 000000010007c318)
STAGE3_COMPONENT_LINKED   = YES  (verified above)
```
