P1 — BOUNDED IN THIS ACT
========================

  Defect:        ctype non-ASCII equivalence was over-claimed
                 (production uses locale-sensitive ctype on
                 plain char; oracle normalizes through
                 unsigned char; the two are not
                 byte-equivalent for bytes >= 0x80 if
                 char is signed).
  Disposition:   B1 contract bounded to ASCII; B1 component
                 must use explicit byte comparisons (no ctype).
  Status:        BOUNDED. The over-claim is removed; the
                 non-ASCII behavior is now classified as
                 P2_DEFERRED residue below.

P2 — DEFERRED RESIDUE (NON-BLOCKING)
====================================

  NON_ASCII_PRODUCTION_BEHAVIOR = P2_DEFERRED

    Production's non-ASCII identifier behavior
    (bytes >= 0x80) is locale-sensitive and possibly
    undefined on signed-char platforms. B1 does not
    claim equivalence.

    Future ACTs may either:
      (a) tighten production to ASCII explicitly, or
      (b) extend B1 to handle non-ASCII per a
          documented locale policy.
    Neither is in B1 scope.
