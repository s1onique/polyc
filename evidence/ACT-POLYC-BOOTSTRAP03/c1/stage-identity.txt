------------------------------------------------------------
Provenance note (binding per §5)
------------------------------------------------------------

stage0 and stage1 produce the **same SHA** for the B1
component object. This is the C1 ACT §5 expected caveat:

> A matching SHA between stage0 and stage1 B1 objects is
> desirable but not sufficient to prove provenance.
> You need causal binding, not merely equal bytes.

Hash equality here is incidental — both stages use the
same host-clang backend and the B1 source is identical,
so the resulting Mach-O is deterministic. The B2
provenance witness must therefore record the **producer
command + output path + stage2 link input**, not just
hashes.
