==========================================

Per ACT §8: the principal RED is **not** "stage1 cannot
compile the B1 source" (B1 already proved that). The
principal RED is:

> The artifact produced by stage1 has not yet been
> consumed to construct a successor compiler stage.

------------------------------------------------------------
Why this is the principal RED
------------------------------------------------------------

B1 demonstrated that a PolyC compiler component can live
inside the compiler. C3 demonstrated that stage1 can also
compile that same source. Neither demonstrated that the
output of stage1 has been consumed to construct a successor
stage.

That is the gap B2 closes.

The fact that the **artifact hash happens to match**
between stage0 and stage1 outputs is incidental — both
use the same host-clang backend, and the B1 source is
deterministic. The B2 ACT explicitly warns against
substituting hash coincidence for provenance (§5).

Provenance for B2 must therefore be recorded as:

```text
producer command line:   ./build/hcc-bootstrap02 -c ... -o <path>
output path:             <canonical absolute path>
artifact hash:           <observed sha256>
stage2 link command:     <CMake + target_link_libraries line>
stage2 symbol table:     nm build/hcc-bootstrap03 | grep _BootstrapScanIdent
stage2 call site:        otool -tv build/hcc-bootstrap03 | grep bl _BootstrapScanIdent
```

All five must align to satisfy the B2 defining invariant.
