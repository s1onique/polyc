------------------------------------------------------------
Interpretation
------------------------------------------------------------

The two `bl _BootstrapScanIdent` instructions at
0x1000303f4 and 0x100031460 are the two delegation sites
in the production lexer:

1. The first site is the **primary `lexIdentifier`**
   path; it is the only call site used for normal token
   stream construction. Its return is checked by the
   following `cbz` (compare-and-branch-on-zero), which
   matches `lexIdentifier`'s contract (`rc == 0` means
   failure).

2. The second site is the **dispatcher fall-through** at
   lexCore (line 1536 of src/lexer.c), where the
   identifier branch hands off to the B1 component
   before any fallback.

The `_BootstrapScanIdent` function body at 0x10007c318 is
the B1 component code itself; its prologue
(`stp x29, x30, [sp, #-0x10]!`) is the standard aarch64
frame setup emitted by hcc for the PolyC B1 source.

Both call sites and the symbol definition are in the
same Mach-O segment of `build/hcc-bootstrap03`. This is
the production binary's static proof that the B1
component is linked and exercised.
