------------------------------------------------------------


All three stages emit byte-identical B1 objects. This is
the strongest determinism witness of the closed
self-host chain:

> Three compiler stages, all carrying the same PolyC B1
> component, when asked to compile the B1 source
> themselves, produce byte-identical output.

The byte-equality is incidental to the structural proof
(provenance = stage1); it is a beneficial side effect of
the deterministic host-clang backend.

------------------------------------------------------------
Why byte equality is NOT the B2 primary witness
------------------------------------------------------------

Per ACT §5:

> A matching SHA between stage0 and stage1 B1 objects is
> desirable but not sufficient to prove provenance.
> You need causal binding, not merely equal bytes.

The B2 primary witness is the build graph:

```text
stage1 -> build/bootstrap03-ident.stage1.o  (record 1)
stage2 -> target_link_libraries(hcc-bootstrap03 ${BOOTSTRAP03_IDENT_OBJECT} m)
         with BOOTSTRAP03_IDENT_OBJECT = build/bootstrap03-ident.stage1.o
```

This evidence file records the **secondary** witness
(byte equality) which is informative but not load-bearing.
