ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP03 — C3 — stage2 self-source
==============================================

Per ACT §24: stage2 (hcc-bootstrap03) compiles the
PolyC B1 component source, and the resulting object
matches stage1's output.

------------------------------------------------------------
Source
------------------------------------------------------------

```text
tools/bootstrap/bootstrap02-ident.HC
```

This is the same PolyC source that constitutes the B1
component. It is written in PolyC (not C), it implements
`BootstrapScanIdent`, and it is linked into both stage1
and stage2.
