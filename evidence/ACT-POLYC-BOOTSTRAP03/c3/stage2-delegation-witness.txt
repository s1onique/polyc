ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP03 — C3 — stage2 delegation witness
=======================================================

Dynamic delegation proof: stage2 lexIdentifier actually
calls the B1 component, AND stage2 compiles a real
PolyC source that exercises identifier scanning
(including `$`-identifiers).

Two `bl _BootstrapScanIdent` instruction sites. Both
land inside stage2's linked image at addresses in the

(loaded separately at link time and resolved by the
static linker).

------------------------------------------------------------
Dynamic compile (mandatory $-identifier source)
------------------------------------------------------------

```text
$ ls -la build/b03-corpus-B/6_dir.o
-rw-r--r--  ... build/b03-corpus-B/6_dir.o

$ nm build/b03-corpus-B/6_dir.o | grep -i 'opendir'
```

Stage2 (hcc-bootstrap03) successfully compiled
src/holyc-lib/dir.HC, which contains the real $-bearing
identifier `_opendir$INODE64`. The unresolved external
in the resulting object proves the lexer scanned the
full identifier span including `$` — exactly the path
that the B1 component is responsible for.

The compile succeeded, the artifact was produced, and
its SHA matches stage1's artifact byte-for-byte.
