=== INTOPS01 native parity ===

Per the recon outcome, there are no new LLVM operations to compare
against native backends. The B0-required operations (IADD, ISUB, IMUL,
ICMP, BR, CALL, RET, LOAD_DEREF, STORE_DEREF) are already proven
semantically consistent with the native AOT and JIT backends by:

- LLVM-SPIKE01-RESUME01 (PASS=18, all scalars)
- CORE04-RESUME01
- MEMORY01 (PASS=6, all derefs)
- FLOAT01 (PASS=29, all floats; not in scope for INTOPS01)

No native integer semantic change has been made by INTOPS01
(this ACT introduced no production code changes; the halt predates IMPL).

PARITY01 remains frozen at CLOSE per ROADMAP P3.7.
