## Semantic question

HolyC `%` follows the underlying machine remainder:
- x86 `idiv` remainder: sign of remainder follows dividend.
- LLVM `srem`: sign of remainder also follows dividend (matches x86).
- LLVM `urem`: standard unsigned remainder.

For positive operands, x86 idiv and LLVM srem agree.
For negative operands:
  - x86: `-7 % 3 = -1` (sign of dividend)
  - LLVM `srem`: `-7 % 3 = -1` (sign of dividend)
  - Mathematical modulo: `-7 mod 3 = 2` (sign of divisor)

So LLVM `srem` happens to match HolyC's machine-level remainder
on x86. But aarch64 backend behavior might differ. The existing
HCC code in src/lexer.c does NOT use `%` on integers.
