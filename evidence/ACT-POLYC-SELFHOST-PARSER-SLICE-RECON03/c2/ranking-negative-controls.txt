controls.  The two families are kept DISTINCT here, as the ACT
requires: the RANKING controls are executed by the PolyC ranker
over the eligible set, and the ELIGIBILITY controls are executed by the

--- the controls are genuinely falsifiable ---
Each control MUTATES one field of one candidate and re-runs the SAME
checker that produces the published verdict.  A control cannot pass
without the predicate reacting to the mutation: NC-08/NC-11/NC-12/NC-13
all assert that the verdict FLIPS, and the driver prints the
baseline verdict so a control that passed for the wrong reason would be
visible in this record.
