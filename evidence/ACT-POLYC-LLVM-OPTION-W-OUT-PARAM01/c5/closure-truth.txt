RED
===
The C5 RED is the binding fixture's documented
ScanIdent(U8 *src, I64 len, I64 cursor, I64 *out_cursor,
I64 *out_len) function body. Pre-C4, this function would
not compile via hcc --emit-llvm because:
  * `ch == ' ' || ch == '\t' || ch == '\n'` lowers to two
    IR_PHI instructions that the prior REJECTED arm
    blocked with LLVM_BACKEND_UNSUPPORTED_PHI.
  * `*out_cursor = cursor` and `*out_len = cursor - start`
    are IR_STORE_DEREF writes requiring Option-W eligibility
    for the cursor mutable local.

C4 IMPL-B is the principal IMPL. C5 verifies it.
