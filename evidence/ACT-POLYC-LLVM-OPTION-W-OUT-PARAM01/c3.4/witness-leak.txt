OUTCOME_F_PRODUCER_LEAK_CONFIRMED

Interpretation for the C4 IR_PHI dispatch arm:
  - The discriminator MUST check the producer opcode,
    not just the LLVM type.
  - A bounded producer-identity seam is required:
      def_map : IrValue id -> defining IrInstr *
    built during llCreateBlocks (or right after) by
    walking every IrInstr in every IrBlock and
    recording instr->dst -> instr.
  - The i1 -> i8 normalization path then becomes:
      producer = def_map_lookup(p->ir_value)
      if !producer || producer->op != IR_ICMP ||
         producer->dst != p->ir_value:
        HALT_PHI_TYPE_CONTRACT_REQUIRED

  - Constants and arguments bypass the producer check
    (they have no defining IrInstr), but they are also
    LLVM i8 by construction (no normalization needed).
    The discriminator naturally falls through to
    "use directly" without ever entering the i1 branch.
