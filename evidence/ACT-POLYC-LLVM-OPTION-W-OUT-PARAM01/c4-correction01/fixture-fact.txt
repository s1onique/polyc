FIXTURE FACT CORRECTION: c4_phi_multi_pred.HC
============================================

Claim in c4/witness-fixtures/c4_phi_multi_pred.HC (line 1-2):

Reality in the emitted witness /tmp/c4_phi_multi_pred.ll:

The three-term OR `a > 0 || b > 0 || c > 0` lowers to TWO
IR_PHI instructions, each with two incoming edges. The
chain:

      bb1: ret 1
      bb3: cmp c > 0; zext; br bb6
      bb5: cmp b > 0; zext; br merge
      bb6: phi1 i8 [ 1, %bb3 ] [ %phi_icmp_zext2, %bb6 ]
              ; constant 1 on bb3 path, icmp zext on bb6 path
      merge: phi i8 [ 1, %bb1 ] [ %phi_icmp_zext, %bb5 ]

is the natural shape: the front-end emits one PHI per merge
block, so a 3-way OR produces two merge blocks (one for the
right sub-OR, one for the overall result).

Both PHIs lower cleanly:
  * hcc             rc=0
  * llvm-as         rc=0
  * opt --passes=verify  rc=0

So the fixture exercises something strictly stronger than
the (incorrect) comment claims: a CHAINED 2-PHI geometry,
not a single 3-input PHI. The fixture still belongs in the
witness set; it is a valid (and slightly harder) case.
