Note on l_start_off:
  The runner captures (l.start - src_buf) for diagnostic
  purposes. The wrapper in src/lexer.c::lexClassifyOperator
  does NOT explicitly mutate l.start; the difference is
  internal to lexCore's normal pre/post cursor logic. The
  production lexer resets l.start = l.ptr at lines 621,
  638, 643 of src/lexer.c after every token, so this
  measurement artifact does not propagate to subsequent
  tokens in production runs. The corpus-evidence
  (175/175 byte-identical, see corpus-4-stages.txt)
  confirms this.
