THIS IS A PLAN. IT IS NOT A RECEIPT AND IT IS NOT A FORECAST.

It describes what WILL be executed AFTER the unique terminal CLOSE
commit. It asserts NOTHING about the outcome of that execution. The
receipt that discharges AC50 will live OUTSIDE the repository, bound to

reported with the receipt, and a new, independently authorized

a second CLOSE is never appended.
