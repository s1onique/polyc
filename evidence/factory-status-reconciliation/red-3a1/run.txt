RED-3A1 — seed silently omits a managed ACT/HANDOFF pair at a
         non-inferred path
==================================================================

Defect:
  scripts/quality/llvm-closure-status-check.sh derives the
  expected evidence directory from the ACT filename via
  act_to_evid_dir(). The function only handles the special-case
  tails "core*" (mapped to llvmspike01-...) and "boundary*"
  (mapped to ir-...). A pair whose ACT filename tail does not
  match any special case is mapped to the lowercased tail as a
  literal evidence directory.

  For ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-NONINFERRED.md the inferred directory is
  evidence/noninferred/. The actual HANDOFF is placed at
  evidence/factory-status-reconciliation/red-3a1/fixture/managed/extra-scope-factory-NON_INFERRED/evidence/handoff-noninfer/HANDOFF.md
  which is not inferable.

  The seed then enters the line:
    [ -f "$handoff" ] || continue
  and silently skips the pair, emitting no diagnostic at all.

Reproduction:
  See evidence/factory-status-reconciliation/red-3a1/run-output.txt
  (captured live at ENTRY_HEAD via /tmp/test_seed_skips.sh which
  replays the seed pairing rule against the two fixture ACTs).
  The relevant lines are:
    OMIT  ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-NONINFERRED.md -> evidence/noninferred/HANDOFF.md (NOT FOUND, seed skips)
    OMIT  ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-MISSINGHO.md   -> evidence/missingho/HANDOFF.md   (NOT FOUND, seed skips)

Demonstration of the defect:
  The seed reports OK=2 FAIL=3 against the real repo (RED-1).
  Adding the two fixtures does not change that count. The
  fixtures are invisible to the seed.

Required treatment:
  Implement scripts/quality/factory-closure-status-check.sh that
  enumerates managed pairs from a manifest bijection
  (docs/factory/act-handoff-map.tsv) rather than inferring them
  from ACT filenames. Then:
    RED-3A2: same pair explicitly listed in fixture manifest
             -> new checker discovers and validates it (PASS).
    RED-3A3: manifest row removed while both files remain
             -> new checker reports UNMAPPED_* and exits
                non-zero (FAIL).

Fixtures:
  ACT     : evidence/factory-status-reconciliation/red-3a1/fixture/managed/extra-scope-factory-NON_INFERRED/docs/acts/ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-NONINFERRED.md
  HANDOFF : evidence/factory-status-reconciliation/red-3a1/fixture/managed/extra-scope-factory-NON_INFERRED/evidence/handoff-noninfer/HANDOFF.md
