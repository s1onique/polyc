VERDICT DISPOSITION:

  The architectural change (manifest-driven enumeration;
  freeze; unmanaged-pair registration; self-row load-bearing)
  is mechanically sound and the gates genuinely PASS at C4.

  The PROOF MACHINERY around it is defective:
    - 1 of 35 mandatory ACs (AC07) backed by an error artifact
    - 1 of 35 mandatory ACs (AC16) fired the wrong counter
    - 1 C4 scope decision (ACT-body ## Status) weakened the
      self-judgment proof
    - 1 narrative claim (F-POLYC-TOOLS) understated a real
      shell test that exceeds the bootstrap-glue cap

  Combined effect: PASS_TRUE_GREEN is not a defensible verdict
  for this ACT. The right verdict is PASS_FALSE_GREEN or
  PASS_ENGINEERING_HALT_EVIDENCE_DEFECTS.

  The closed ACT (commits f8ae7ac..985ebbd) is historical
  evidence per F14. The closed HANDOFF claims PASS_TRUE_GREEN
  and CANNOT be modified. The correct next step is to:

    1. Open ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-CLOSURE-ORACLE-EXTENSIBLE01-
       CORRECTION01.
    2. In that ACT:
       a. Re-run AC07 with a properly populated baseline
          manifest and capture real evidence.
       b. Re-run N06 with a manifest constructed so the
          DUPLICATE_PAIRS counter fires (e.g., the duplicate
          ACT path is paired with a different HANDOFF path,

          DUPLICATE_PAIRS is always computed).

       e. The HANDOFF for this CORRECTION01 should claim

          PASS_ENGINEERING_HALT_EVIDENCE_DEFECTS, not
