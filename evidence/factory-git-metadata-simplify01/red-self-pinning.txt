RED-1 SELF-PINNING RED
======================

PATTERN=self_pinning
REPRODUCED=YES

Historical witness: ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-STATUS-RECONCILIATION-CORRECTION02

  commit 849677ade3d6275996409846bbe99be49c6145b1
  evidence/factory-status-reconciliation-correction02/HANDOFF.md
  lines 28-33:

    This ACT's IDENTITY chain (CORRECTION02):

    ENTRY_HEAD (CORRECTION02)  = 2d7ff45de22b826abb3096ed611cd7ac02f05134
    RED_HEAD   (CORRECTION02)  = b62f940 <C1 RED commit for this ACT>
    IMPL_HEAD  (CORRECTION02)  = <C2 IMPL commit; this commit>
    DOCS_HEAD  (CORRECTION02)  = <C3 DOCS commit; committed in the next commit>

This is exactly the self-pinning pattern Factory v2 forbids:

  * "this commit"                 -- a textual placeholder that
                                     resolves only against the
                                     commit that contains the
                                     placeholder itself; the SHA
                                     is by definition impossible
                                     to write into the document
                                     before the document exists.
  * "committed in the next commit" -- a forward-reference to a
                                     SHA that does not exist yet
                                     and whose identity will
                                     change if any byte of C2 or
                                     C3 is amended.
  * "<C1 RED commit for this ACT>" -- a placeholder using the
                                     ACT's commit topology rather
                                     than Git's commit identity.

The defect became observable at the next ACT. The committed
CORRECTION02 HANDOFF at C3 contained a bound ENTRY_HEAD and
RED_HEAD but unbound IMPL_HEAD and DOCS_HEAD. Two new commits
(8888efb = C1 RED of CORRECTION03 and 29fb9afc = C2 CLEAN of
CORRECTION03) had to be authored entirely to repair the
self-pinning defect left in 849677a. The repair ACT is
ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-STATUS-RECONCILIATION-CORRECTION03; its
HANDOFF (also committed) explicitly documents:

    "This intentionally avoids the
     '<C2 SHA hard-baked at C2 commit time>' self-pinning loop
     that this ACT is correcting."

Other historical examples (illustrative, not exhaustive):
the IR-BOUNDARY03-CORRECTION02 HANDOFF pinned
FINAL_HEAD = 68ce2bf033428e0e26c984890741f1eb6eb101d2 with a
"DRIFT NOTE: may be off by 1-2 amend iterations", explicitly
acknowledged as P0-2 by IR-BOUNDARY03-CORRECTION03 § "P0-2:
FINAL_HEAD self-pinning (amend-loop residual)".

Many ACTs under docs/acts/ continue to embed ENTRY_HEAD,
FINAL_HEAD, DOCS_HEAD, CLOSURE_HEAD, or IMPLEMENTATION_HEAD
SHA fields in their bodies. They are LEGACY_ONLY per V2-13
and not rewritten by this ACT. The negative scan in ACT §13
is scoped to Factory-v2 templates and new docs only.

Conclusion:
  Git, not Markdown, owns commit identity.
  v2 forbids FINAL_HEAD/CLOSURE_HEAD/DOCS_HEAD/IMPLEMENTATION_HEAD
  SHA fields that purport to identify the commit containing
  them. Self-pinning loops are mechanically unrepresentable
  in v2 because Git trailers carry phase/verdict, not the
  SHA of the committing commit.
