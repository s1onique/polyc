These two tokens are independently authored, both claim to be
"the verdict", and they disagree. The factory-closure-status
checker (CORRECTION01/CORRECTION02/CORRECTION03) was created
explicitly to detect this drift on a bounded managed universe
of ACT/HANDOFF pairs. Until that checker existed, the
ACT/HANDOFF pair was checked only by reviewer inspection.

The reviewer also caught a deeper false-GREEN at
ACT-POLYC-LLVM-CORE03-CORRECTION05 (commit 862d2cc):
the ACT introduced a hard gate (closure-status-check) and
declared PASS, but the new gate was RED at the very tree
where the ACT declared PASS (OK=2 FAIL=3). This is recorded
in evidence/llvmspike01-core03-correction05/HANDOFF.md:

    "Yet the ACT simultaneously records:
        A5 = PASS
        A8 = PASS
        VERDICT = PASS
     That is a direct false-GREEN under the ACT's own
     newly-created oracle."

Both defects have the same shape: two documents
(## Status vs VERDICT) each claim to be the verdict, with
no single mechanical source of truth. They drift.

The CORRECTION01/02/03/04 chain was the entire reason the
factory-closure-status oracle exists today, and even that
checker (still v1) is bolted on rather than replacing the
duplicated authority. Its hard-coded managed universe is
still only 6 pairs. Factory v1 has had to expand it manually
each time a new ACT/HANDOFF pair needs reconciliation.

Factory v2 solution: one ACT-verdict source = the CLOSE
commit's ACT-Verdict trailer. ACT documents and HANDOFFs are
descriptive only; they may not contain a mutable verdict
field. Historical ACT/HANDOFFs remain grandfathered (V2-13).
