RED-3 ARBITRARY-COMMIT-CAP RED
=============================

PATTERN=arbitrary_commit_cap
REPRODUCED=YES

The RED is not "many commits are good". The RED is:

  An arbitrary numeric topology rule (typically the
  RED + IMPL + DOCS = 3 commits contract) caused otherwise
  bounded, auditable ACTs to require a process correction
  unrelated to product correctness.

Two historical cases (load-bearing examples; many more exist
under docs/acts/):

------------------------------------------------------------
Case A: ACT-POLYC-IR-BOUNDARY03-CORRECTION02
------------------------------------------------------------
  Declared cap (in ACT-POLYC-IR-BOUNDARY03-CORRECTION02.md
  §9): 3 commits.

  Actual honest commits in the chain (from CORRECTION03 §P0-1):
    cbc0e00, 0971c4d, e4b66f7, 46a65c2  =  4 commits.

  The fourth commit (46a65c2) was an evidence-only
  amendment to make the closure-status-check output
  truthful; it was not product code. The ACT's own §9
  declared the 3-commit cap and AC-12 said
  "CORRECTION02 commit cap = 3", but the work
  honestly required one more commit.

  Outcome: CORRECTION03 was opened exclusively to acknowledge
  the breach ("P0-1: cap-violation (3 vs 4)") and to mark
  the historical act as PASS_WITH_TOPOLOGY_BREACH rather
  than silently rewrite history. No product defect;
  purely a process bookkeeping correction.

------------------------------------------------------------
Case B: ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-STATUS-RECONCILIATION-CORRECTION02
------------------------------------------------------------
  Declared cap: 3 commits (RED + IMPL + DOCS+HANDOFF).
  Actual honest commits: 3 (b62f940, d9b9961, 849677a).
  But the C3 commit's HANDOFF left two of its own identity
  fields (IMPL_HEAD, DOCS_HEAD) as "<this commit>" and
  "<committed in the next commit>" placeholders -- the
  exact self-pinning defect recorded in red-self-pinning.txt.
  Removing those placeholders truthfully required another
  commit, which exceeded the cap, so CORRECTION03 was
  opened to repair the closure-state defect.

  Outcome: two commits (8888efb + 29fb9afc) of
  CORRECTION03 exist entirely to repair the CORRECTION02
  HANDOFF's identity placeholders. No product defect;
  purely a process bookkeeping correction.

------------------------------------------------------------
Conclusion
------------------------------------------------------------
  Both cases share the same shape:

    declared numeric cap < actual honest commits
    excess commit existed only because the closure
        evidence / status reconciliation / amendment work
        was honestly preserved
    the excess caused a process correction ACT
        (with its own review cycle, its own HANDOFF,
        its own residue, its own gate wiring)
        unrelated to product correctness

  Factory v2's response: there is no numeric commit cap.
  The closure topology is bounded by ACT scope, not by an
  integer. ACT §V2-7 replaces the rule explicitly:

    "Commits must be bounded by ACT scope, contiguous,
     honestly classified, and individually meaningful."

  T14 (RED matrix §12) load-bears this: a 5-commit synthetic
  ACT (RED, IMPL, EVIDENCE, IMPL, CLOSE) is valid under v2.

  This is not a license for unbounded growth; it is a
  rejection of arbitrary integer caps that force honest
  process work into either silent history rewriting or
  yet another correction ACT.
