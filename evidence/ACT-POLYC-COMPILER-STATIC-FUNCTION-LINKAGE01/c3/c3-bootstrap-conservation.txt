    independent symbol gaps recorded in the LEXER04-CORRECTION01
    HALT and explicitly excluded from this ACT per ACT §1.

    not involve fn_is_static propagation; src/lexer.c was not
    touched. Its preservation is therefore guaranteed by code
    locality, not by re-execution.

  - Native object correctness is mechanically proven by S01..S08
    fresh execution (c3-s01..c3-s08 evidence files).
