Why this is correct (and CORE01 must document it):

The neutral IR shape (per src/ir-types.h and src/ir-eval.c) is that
IR_BR's `dst` is an i64 condition value (0 or 1). LLVM's
LLVMBuildCondBr wants an i1. Therefore the truncation is a
deliberate neutral->LLVM boundary crossing: the neutral IR uses
i64 for condition carriers (uniform with the scalar i64 type
language), and the LLVM IR needs i1 for condbr.

A future contributor might "fix" this by changing the IR_BR `dst`
type to i1, which would change the neutral IR contract and
invalidate downstream consumers (notably the native backend).
CORE01 documents the existing behaviour as deliberate so the
intent is preserved.

CORE01 fix: add a paragraph immediately above the LLVMBuildTrunc
call explaining:

  1. The neutral IR carries IR_BR conditions as i64.
  2. LLVM condbr wants i1.
  3. The truncation is a deliberate boundary crossing.
  4. Do NOT change the neutral IR contract to fix this.
