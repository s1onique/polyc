Analysis:

  The 6 trailing-whitespace lines are compiler-emitted output from
  the legacy x86 backend. They come from internal compiler comment
  markers ("# INt arithmetic START" and "# INt arithmetic END")
  with a single trailing space. These markers are produced by the
  legacy backend's codegen, not by hand-written code.

  The trailing space is part of the legitimate compiler output. It
  is evidence of what the compiler produced, not a defect introduced
  by the agent.

  Suppressing the trailing whitespace would falsify the evidence
  (the sanitizer file is preserved in c3-reverify/c3-reverify.S.sanitized.txt
  for diff-check cleanliness, alongside the raw file in c3-reverify/c3-reverify.S.raw.txt).

  The C2+C3 commit added 6 trailing-whitespace lines. They are an
  honest artifact of compiler capture, not a hygiene defect in the
  agent-authored patch. The "PATCH_HYGIENE = FAIL" disposition in
  the HANDOFF reflects git-diff-check's mechanical verdict, not a
  claim that the C2 src/x86.c production change contains
  whitespace errors (it does not).

Forward-looking repair:

  The principled future path is to teach the static-function-linkage-test
  capture step to strip trailing whitespace from compiler output before
  writing evidence files (e.g., `sed 's/[[:space:]]*$//'` or `tr -d ' '`).
  This can be a small bounded change to scripts/quality/static-function-linkage-test.sh
  in a future ACT, not this ACT.

  Until then, the c3/ .S.txt file is committed AS-IS and the trailing-
  whitespace lines are documented residue.
