ACT-POLYC-SELFHOST-DRIVER-REQUIRED-ROUTE-CORRECTION01 -- TERMINALITY PLAN
=========================================================================

THIS IS A PLAN. IT IS NOT A RECEIPT AND IT IS NOT A FORECAST.

It describes what WILL be executed AFTER the unique terminal CLOSE
commit. It asserts NOTHING about the outcome of that execution. The
receipt that discharges AC50 will live OUTSIDE the repository, bound to
the CLOSE HEAD and TREE, and its result will be whatever it actually
is. A failure of the external probe is an ACTUAL FAILURE: it is
reported with the receipt, and a new, independently authorized
forward-only correction is requested. It is never recast as green, and
a second CLOSE is never appended.

  * No second CLOSE will be appended, whatever the probe's result.
  * No historical ACT, HANDOFF or evidence file will be rewritten.
