ACT-POLYC-SELFHOST-DRIVER-REQUIRED-ROUTE-CORRECTION01 -- C4 EVIDENCE MANIFEST
=========================================================================

THE C4 EVIDENCE LIST, AND WHY IT IS EXTENDED.

The ACT's section 7 names exactly ONE C4 evidence file:

  c4/
    terminality-plan.txt

The ACT permits extending that list "only inside this ACT's evidence
namespace, with provenance and a manifest". This file IS that
manifest, and it names every C4 evidence file, its provenance, and why
it is present.

THE FILES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

  terminality-plan.txt
    provenance  the C4 CLOSE's own prospective plan
    why         REQUIRED BY THE ACT. It states what the external
                read-only probe WILL verify and states explicitly that
                it asserts nothing about the probe's outcome.

  acceptance-ledger-ac01-ac50-final.tsv
    provenance  the C3 ledger, re-adjudicated at the CLOSE
    why         The ACT says "At C4 AC48 and AC49 may become PASS after
                their own obligations execute, while AC50 remains
                DEFERRED until the external probe. There may be no
                unsupported PASS." The C3 ledger recorded the C3
                phase-local status and could not record the CLOSE
                status without being rewritten, which F14 forbids for
                a committed evidence file. A NEW C4 ledger records
                the final state, and the C3 ledger is left untouched
                as the historical record of the C3 phase.

  manifest.txt
    provenance  this file
    why         REQUIRED BY THE EXTENSION CLAUSE.

WHAT IS NOT IN THIS DIRECTORY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

  NO RECEIPT. The external terminality receipt lives OUTSIDE the
  repository. It is not committed here, it is not forecast here, and
  it does not exist yet.

  NO PASS FOR AC50. The final ledger records AC50 as DEFERRED. It is
  discharged by the external receipt, not by a committed forecast.

  NO SECOND CLOSE. This directory is committed once, by the unique
  terminal CLOSE.

THE FINAL LEDGER'S DISPOSITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

  AC01..AC47   PASS, unchanged from C3, with their C3 evidence paths
  AC48         PASS  (the unique terminal CLOSE exists; the five phase
                      commits carry valid parseable trailers; no commit
                      continues this ACT id)
  AC49         PASS  (the HANDOFF, the handoff-map entry and the
                      roadmap append distinguish the route PASS from the
                      global remainder and from the historical AC debt;
                      no receipt is pre-recorded)
  AC50         DEFERRED (the external probe has not run)

  AC_PASS_AT_CLOSE            49
  AC_DEFERRED_AT_CLOSE         1
  AC_UNSUPPORTED_PASS          0
