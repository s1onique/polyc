===============================

the PolyC delegation (HCC_USE_SELFHOST_PARSER_AST_BINOP) and the
retained legacy switch (the #else branch).

authorization was required and no semantic conservation witness for
the unary function is owed.

THE STAGE0 BUILD IS NOT CYCLIC
==============================

The stage0 compiler builds with EVERY delegation OFF, including the
new one:

  -DHCC_USE_SELFHOST_PARSER_AST_BINOP=OFF

so the stage0 compiler does not require the PolyC subject that the
subject's own build needs to produce it.  The bootstrap chain stays
acyclic:

  host C compiler
        |
        v
  bootstrap-stage0            build/host-stage0/hcc
        |                      (every delegation OFF)
        v
  parser-ast-binop-subject    build/parser-ast-binop-subject.o
        |                      (compiled by the stage0 compiler)
        v
  production hcc               ./hcc
                               (HCC_USE_SELFHOST_PARSER_AST_BINOP=ON)

  BOOTSTRAP_CYCLE=NO
