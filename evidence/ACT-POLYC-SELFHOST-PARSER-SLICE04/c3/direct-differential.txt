WHAT THE FOUR ASSERTIONS MEAN
-----------------------------

For every one of the 322 fixture rows the differential asserts:

  LEGACY_SUCCESS=POLYC_SUCCESS
  LEGACY_OUTPUT=POLYC_OUTPUT
  EXPECTED_SUCCESS=LEGACY_SUCCESS
  EXPECTED_OUTPUT=LEGACY_OUTPUT

and, for the invalid tokens, additionally:

  the production adapter preserves the caller's initial output value

The counters above are the MEASURED counts of those assertions.  A
mismatch in any of them is a non-zero counter and a FAIL status.

THE TWO AUTHORITIES
-------------------

  legacy: Slice04LegacyBinOpOracle -> the REAL astBinOpFromToken, in
          build/slice04-ast-legacy.o (src/ast.c compiled WITHOUT the
          delegation macro)
  polyc:  BootstrapAstBinOpFromToken, in
          build/parser-ast-binop-subject.o

The two are compared on EVERY row.  The fixture's own expectation is
also checked against the oracle, so a disagreement between the PolyC
subject and the production oracle cannot be masked by an equally wrong
fixture: SLICE04_DIFF_ORACLE_VS_EXPECTED_MISMATCH is 0.

NO ROW IS SKIPPED
-----------------

A row that cannot be parsed is a FAILURE, never a silent skip:
SLICE04_DIFF_PARSE_ERRORS and SLICE04_DIFF_FIELD_PARSE_ERRORS are both
0, and the acceptance predicate requires them to be 0.  The fixture's
canonical ID set is F001..F322, every id occurs exactly once, and the
observed row count equals the expected row count, which is DERIVED
from the fixture file itself rather than hard-coded.

THE SIGNED-I64 EXTREMES ARE EXERCISED, NOT WRAPPED
----------------------------------------------------

The M06 partition carries INT64_MIN, INT64_MIN+1, INT64_MIN+2,
INT64_MAX-2, INT64_MAX-1 and INT64_MAX.  The differential's integer
parser is EXACT over the whole signed 64-bit domain:

  * the magnitude is accumulated as a NEGATIVE I64, so the magnitude
    2^63 (needed by INT64_MIN) is exactly representable;
  * the pre-multiply limit is checked BEFORE each multiply;
  * the post-multiply floor is checked AFTER each multiply;
  * and the 19-digit case is resolved by the sign-aware magnitude
    bound, because INT64_MAX and INT64_MIN BOTH have nineteen digits
    and BOTH are in range.

The out-of-range literal 9223372036854775808 is REJECTED by that parser
rather than silently wrapping: that is the subject of the NC-05
negative control, which is a genuine rejection by the real verifier.
