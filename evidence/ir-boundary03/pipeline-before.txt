KEY ARCHITECTURAL OBSERVATION
    The LLVM dispatch path:
        main.c:561  irLowerProgram(cc)
        ir.c:3380   -> per func: irLowerFunction + irBasicFunctionOptimisations
        llvmEmitProgram(prog, ...)   <-- IrProgram is consumed here
    NEVER enters irFunctionPrepForCodeGen and therefore
    NEVER creates IR_CMP_BR. The LLVM backend's IR_CMP_BR
    switch arm at src/llvm-backend.c:610-636 is DEAD CODE
    in the current source.

    This contradicts the literal RED-1A premise in the
    predecessor C1 ACT, which claimed the LLVM consumer
    walks IR after native fusion. The fusion is NOT
    triggered on the LLVM path.
