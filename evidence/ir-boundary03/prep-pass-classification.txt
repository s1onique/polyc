LLVM consumer notes:
    - LLVM consumer receives the IR after the FIRST TEN
      neutral passes via irLowerProgram (which calls
      irBasicFunctionOptimisations).
    - LLVM does NOT call any of the NATIVE_ONLY passes.
    - The neutral pipeline already covers the public
      semantic-transformation needs of LLVM (DCE, const-fold,
      store-to-read forwarding, fold-passthrough-blocks,
      etc.). The LLVM consumer does not need any native-only
      pass to lower canonical IR_ICMP + IR_BR.

--dump-ir notes:
    - --dump-ir calls irBasicFunctionOptimisations directly
      (after irLowerFunction), which runs the same ten
      neutral passes.
    - --dump-ir shows the IR the LLVM consumer will receive.
    - --dump-ir does NOT call any NATIVE_ONLY pass; physical
      registers / stack offsets / IR_CMP_BR are never
      shown in the dump.
