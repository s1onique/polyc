ACT-POLYC-IR-BOUNDARY03 / Truthiness matrix (ACT §15, §16)
=========================================================

Source: /tmp/truthiness_main.HC (self-contained: BranchOn
+ main that runs all six discriminators and prints results).

The BranchOn function lowers to:

    cmp_ne  %t i8, %v, 0
    br      %t, true_bb, false_bb

when the condition is not a preceding CMP (which it isn't,
since `v` is the parameter). This is case 2 of the
branch-condition-producers.txt analysis.

The lowering inserts an explicit IR_ICMP(NE, v, 0) coercion,
producing an I8 result. The IR representation is "truthiness
of the I64 parameter captured as I8 {0, 1}".
