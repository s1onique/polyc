Role taxonomy for inventory.sh (c1/inventory.txt)
==================================================

TEST      per-test harness (llvm-*-test.sh, native-*-test.sh,
          ir-return-slot-*-test.sh, harness-evidence-*-test.sh)

GATE      gate runner (gate-fast.sh, gate-push.sh, llvm-closure-*-check.sh)

FACTORY   factory v1/v2 evidence machinery
          (factory-*-test.sh, factory-*-check.sh,
           factory-v2-*.sh)

TOOL      general-purpose tool that does not fit the above
          (inventory.sh, shell-loc-gate.sh)

Role is informational. The 50 LOC ratchet applies regardless
of role.
