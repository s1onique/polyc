## Determinism check (same production binary, repeated execution)

```text
fixture_alignof distinct_output_sha256_over_3_runs=1
fixture_u0_tail distinct_output_sha256_over_3_runs=1
fixture_inheritance distinct_output_sha256_over_3_runs=1
```

A value of 1 means the fixture output is deterministic: three
consecutive executions of the same production binary produced the
identical byte stream. This is what makes the six-way comparison
a semantic comparison rather than a flaky one.
