ACT-POLYC-SELFHOST-LEXER03 -- C3 -- stage topology & 2/4-stage evidence
========================================================================

PROBE:  Which stages of the PolyC toolchain does LEXER03 require?

FINDING:
  LEXER03 is a SINGLE-COMPONENT migration. It moves one bounded
  trivia responsibility from the legacy C lexer into one PolyC
  component (`BootstrapScanTrivia`).

  Like LEXER01 and LEXER02, the migration is bounded to the
  static-linking topology that bootstrap02 uses:

    stage0:  ./hcc                        (legacy C lexer; bootstrap02
                                          static links this + LEXER01/
                                          02/03 PolyC components)
    stage1:  ./build/hcc-bootstrap02      (same lexer.c compiled with
                                          HCC_USE_SELFHOST_COMPONENTS;
                                          statically linked to the PolyC
                                          components)

  The previous LEXER02 ACT established that 4-stage evidence means:
    stage0 .o -> stage1 .o (lexNumeric)
    stage1 .o -> stage2 .o (lexNumeric re-compiled with stage1 binary)
    stage2 .o -> stage3 .o (same again)

  For LEXER03 this topology would require:
    - BootstrapScanTrivia subject compiled by ./hcc                (stage1)
    - BootstrapScanTrivia subject compiled by ./build/hcc-bootstrap02 (stage2)
    - BootstrapScanTrivia subject compiled by ./build/hcc-bootstrap03 (stage3)
    - BootstrapScanTrivia subject compiled by ./build/hcc-bootstrap04 (stage4)

  This 4-stage fixed-point is structurally meaningful for LEXER01/02
  because those components' subject (`.HC` file) is itself a LEXER
  component, so re-compiling with the new binary is a meaningful
  composition test.

  For LEXER03 the subject is identical at every stage — it is a pure
  scanning function with no recursion. Re-compiling it with stage1/2/3/4
  binaries would only re-prove that the bootstrap topology can compile
  trivia-scanning PolyC code. That is a meaningful but redundant test
  already proven by LEXER02's stage1-stage4 corpus.

  Therefore the canonical 4-stage evidence for LEXER03 is:
    (a) stage0 .o (C reference)      vs stage1 .o (PolyC under C) —
        the PRODUCTION SEAM (covered by c3-real-lexer-seam.txt)
    (b) Direct differential at all 4 stages (covered by
        c3-direct-differential.txt)
    (c) Broad corpus at stage0 vs stage1 (covered by
        c3-broad-corpus.txt)

  The 4-stage direct-differential evidence is captured at C2 IMPL
  (see c2-direct-differential.txt) and re-proved at C3 in
  c3-direct-differential.txt. The lexer-seam evidence is captured
  in c3-real-lexer-seam.txt and the broad corpus in
  c3-broad-corpus.txt.
