The 'PASS at HEAD 4e192f2' string pins a SHA that is not the
final commit of the closure range. 4e192f2 was an intermediate
DOCS commit that was subsequently amended (HEAD became 708d620
after the status-fix amend). The identity anti-pattern is
this:

    closure document embeds "current HEAD"
        \/
    document gets committed/amended
        \/
    HEAD changes
        \/
    document is stale at the instant it closes

The fix is not 'update it to 708d620' -- that just recreates
the recursion. The Status block must not self-reference the
commit containing the relationship. Use:

    Status: PASS
    IDENTITY:
        ENTRY_HEAD=<fixed predecessor>
        RED_HEAD=<fixed>
        IMPLEMENTATION_HEAD=<fixed>
        FINAL_HEAD = reviewer resolves from repository topology
