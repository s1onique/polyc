THE PROVENANCE MODEL
=====================

A generation's provenance is the PAIR (component object SHA-256,
producing compiler SHA-256).

The object SHA alone is NOT sufficient, and this control MEASURED
that: G1, G2 and G3 are byte-identical by construction, so an
object-SHA-only check ACCEPTS a substitution of one of them for
another.  The compiler SHA is what distinguishes them, because G1, G2
and G3 are produced by THREE DIFFERENT compilers.

COMMAND=make parser-slice03-copy-forgery-control
OUTPUT:
  SLICE03_FORGERY=CONTROL

    G2_OBJECT_SHA256=1a932394ba209267ab3a119137f844682f1a12ceeaaca0cde4feaeaa0f8f62f8
    G2_COMPILER_SHA256=051bf6b8a74f31a6dbbe97d89bb8d619d53d17e25e3d15d4ab9b89c8521ade38

Without such a control, a provenance check that accepts anything
would report a pass.
