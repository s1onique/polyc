ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-NO-SHA-OF-SELF01-CORRECTION04 — Cardinality-1-CLOSE invariant

=== STATEMENT ===

For any ACT id present in the repository's Git history, the
set of commits carrying both:

  ACT: <ACT-id>
  ACT-Phase: CLOSE

MUST have cardinality exactly 1.

This is the Cardinality-1 CLOSE invariant. It holds for all
ACTs going forward.

=== WHY ===

The Cardinality-1 invariant gives `git log --all-match -1`
a well-defined meaning: there is exactly ONE CLOSE commit,
and `-1` selects it without ambiguity. The SHA obtained is
"the SHA of the closure of ACT-X" — a stable, mechanically
queryable identity.

If Cardinality != 1:
  - 0: the ACT has no closure commit; downstream tools that
    query for the closure SHA fail
  - >=2: the ACT has multiple CLOSE commits; `-1` silently
    picks one, hiding the invariant violation from view

=== ENFORCEMENT ===

The Factory Cardinality-Check (post-FACTORY-CLOSE-CARDINALITY01,
not in this session) SHOULD verify this invariant by
enumerating all ACT ids and counting their CLOSE commits:

```sh
for act_id in $(git log --pretty=format:'%H %(trailers:key=ACT,valueonly)' \
                       | grep -oE 'ACT: ACT-POLYC-[A-Z0-9-]+' \
                       | sort -u); do
    count=$(git log --all-match \
                    --grep="^${act_id}\$" \
                    --grep='^ACT-Phase: CLOSE$' \
                    --oneline | wc -l)
    if [ "$count" -ne 1 ]; then
        echo "VIOLATION: $act_id has $count CLOSE commits (expected 1)"
    fi
done
```

(This snippet is illustrative; the real check would handle
trailer format variations.)

=== GOING FORWARD ===

For future ACTs opened after this ACT closes:

  Hygiene follow-ups MUST carry `ACT-Phase: EVIDENCE` (or
  a non-CLOSE phase), NOT `ACT-Phase: CLOSE`. They are
  follow-ups to the same ACT, not separate closures.

  Material changes that warrant a new bounded correction
  ACT should be opened as `ACT-N-CORRECTIONK` with its own
  CLOSE trailer.

  Pre-existing violations (listed in
  `historical-cardinality-exceptions.txt`) are F14
  historical residue; they are documented but not "fixed"
  by re-mutation.
