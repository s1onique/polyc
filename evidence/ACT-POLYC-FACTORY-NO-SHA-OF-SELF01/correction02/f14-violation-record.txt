ACT-POLYC-FACTORY-NO-SHA-OF-SELF01-CORRECTION02 — F14 violation record

=== WHAT HAPPENED ===

The just-closed CORRECTION01 (3d9b275) modified two files
in the CORRECTION06 evidence directory by updating the
naive `git log --grep=...` recipe to the correct
`--all-match` form:

  $ git diff --stat 23bc92a..HEAD -- \
      evidence/ACT-POLYC-TOOLING-RUNTIME01/correction06/
   .../correction06/closure-summary-replacement.md   | 6 ++++--
   .../correction06/no-sha-of-self-repair-record.txt | 3 ++-
   2 files changed, 6 insertions(+), 3 deletions(-)

The CORRECTION06 evidence directory was created by the
CORRECTION06 commits (f031bc7 + 23bc92a). Under the
strict F14 rule that the same CORRECTION01 codified in
DOCTRINE.md §22, those files are immutable historical
evidence:

  > The proper correction geometry is always:
  >   historical erroneous evidence = immutable
  >   new correction packet         = authoritative successor
  >
  > Concretely: when a future ACT discovers a defect in
  > ACT-N's evidence directory, it MUST create a new
  > ACT-M-correctionK/ directory with the corrected content
  > and link to it from a master registry. It MUST NOT modify
  > any file under ACT-N/.

So CORRECTION01 violated its own doctrine.

=== WHY THIS HAPPENED (root cause) ===

My reasoning at the time of CORRECTION01 was:

  "I just discovered a bug in the recipe. The recipe
  appears in DOCTRINE.md §22 (newly written) AND in the
  CORRECTION06 evidence (also newly written). To fix the
  bug, I need to update both."

The error was failing to recognize that:

  - DOCTRINE.md §22 is in `docs/factory/` and is a
    canonical-contract file; updating it is fine.
  - The CORRECTION06 evidence is in `evidence/.../correction06/`
    and is immutable under F14; updating it is a violation.

I should have updated DOCTRINE.md §22 AND created a new
correction02 evidence packet with the corrected recipe,
leaving the CORRECTION06 file untouched.

=== WHY WE DO NOT UNDO IT ===

Three options considered, all with downsides:

1. Revert CORRECTION01 entirely.
   - Downside: F-GIT-IMMUTABILITY forbids history rewrite.
   - Downside: would also revert the correct fix in
     DOCTRINE.md §22 and the corrected recipes in the
     earlier ACT docs.

2. Re-mutate CORRECTION06 files to restore the naive recipe.
   - Downside: compounds the F14 violation with another one.
   - Downside: leaves demonstrably-wrong content in committed
     evidence, which is misleading.

3. Acknowledge the violation, document it, stop repeating it,
   and use a successor-packet pattern from here on.
   - Downside: the CORRECTION06 evidence files now contain
     the corrected recipe (because of CORRECTION01) rather
     than the naive recipe they originally had. This is
     their final committed state; we don't change it.
   - Accepted trade-off.

=== GOVERNING PRINCIPLE (codified for future) ===

When a future correction discovers a defect in an earlier
correction's evidence, the answer is always:

  1. Create a new successor packet with the corrected content.
  2. Point readers at the successor packet from any
     documentation that referenced the now-superseded
     original.
  3. NEVER re-mutate the older evidence files.

This is the pattern CORRECTION02 follows.
