inspect the last 4 bytes via xxd:

$ tail -c 4 evidence/.../c1/prior-negative-score-red.txt | xxd

$ tail -c 4 evidence/.../c1/selfhost-surface-baseline.txt | xxd
00000000: 292e 0a0a                                )...

$ tail -c 4 evidence/.../c4/final-region-map.txt | xxd
00000000: 732e 0a0a                                s...

character `.` followed by TWO line-feed (LF / 0x0a) bytes.

$ tail -c 4 evidence/.../c2/winner.txt | xxd
00000000: 7374 290a                                st).

to the EXTRA 0x0a byte, not to removal of the final 0x0a.
