NC-P01 -- THE G1 WITNESS PAYLOAD IN THE G3 SLOT
===============================================

THE CONTROL
------------
COMMAND=make parser-slice05-provenance-controls
OUTPUT:
    NC_P01_GENERATION_ROWS=4
    NC_P01_GENERATION=g0
    NC_P01_GENERATION=g1
    NC_P01_GENERATION=g2
    NC_P01_GENERATION=g3
    NC_P01_OTHER_ROWS_UNCHANGED=YES
  SLICE05_FORGERY=CASE-NC-P01 the G1 witness payload in the G3 slot (four generations present)
    SLICE05_FORGERY=CASE-NC-P01 PROVENANCE_VERDICT=REJECTED

  The control starts from a COMPLETE four-generation witness and
  replaces ONLY the G3 block's payload with the G1 block's payload.
  The G3 label is retained.

THE COMPLETENESS PRECONDITIONS
-----------------------------
  NC_G3_SUBSTITUTION_MANIFEST_COMPLETE=YES
    GENERATION_ROWS=4  (the mutant still carries four rows)
    STAGE_LABELS=G0,G1,G2,G3  (all four labels are present and in
    order; the g3 label was NOT changed)
    ALL_REQUIRED_RECEIPTS_PRESENT=YES  (the mutant still carries four
    PROBE_EXECUTION_OUTPUT lines, because the substituted G1 payload
    carries the G1 receipt)
  NC_G3_SUBSTITUTION_CHANGED_TARGET=G3
  NC_G3_SUBSTITUTION_OTHER_ROWS_UNCHANGED=YES
    (the g0, g1 and g2 blocks are byte-for-byte identical between
     the pristine witness and the mutant -- verified with cmp)

THE REJECTION
--------------
  NC_G3_SUBSTITUTION_REJECTED=YES
  NC_G3_SUBSTITUTION_REJECTION_REASON=G3_BINDING_MISMATCH

  THE VERIFIER OUTPUT:
COMMAND=cat build/slice05-nc/nc-p01.txt
  GENERATIONS_PRESENT=4
  GENERATION_ROWS=4
  STAGE_LABELS_MATCH=4
  G3_BINDING_MISMATCH g3 recorded binding [STAGE1_POLYC] but the expected binding is [STAGE3_POLYC]
  G3_STAGE_IDENTITY_MISMATCH g3 recorded run binding [STAGE1_POLYC] but the expected stage identity is [STAGE3_POLYC]
  ALL_REQUIRED_RECEIPTS_PRESENT=4
  REJECT_DUPLICATE_NONCE the witness carries the same nonce for two generations
  DUPLICATE_NONCE_COUNT=1
  GENERATIONS_VERIFIED=4
  PROVENANCE_VERDICT=REJECTED

  The reason is the G3-SPECIFIC BINDING mismatch: the G3 row
  carries the G1 binding (STAGE1_POLYC) and the expected binding
  for the G3 row is STAGE3_POLYC.

  A ROW-COUNT, MISSING-GENERATION, or MALFORMED-INPUT rejection would
  NOT have satisfied this control.  The row count is 4, the labels
  are g0,g1,g2,g3, and the witness parses cleanly.  The ONLY
  thing wrong is the G3 BINDING, and that is exactly what the
  verifier names.

  The DUPLICATE_NONCE diagnostic is ALSO reported, because the
  substituted G1 payload carries the G1 nonce, so two generations
  now share it.  The verifier does NOT SHORT-CIRCUIT: it reports
  EVERY diagnostic it finds, which is why the intended G3-scoped
  reason is present alongside it.

THE PRISTINE RECHECK
-------------------
  PRISTINE_VERDICT=PASS
  PRISTINE_RECHECK=PASS
  PRISTINE_MANIFEST_VALID=YES

THE COMPARISON WITH THE CLOSED SLICE04 CONTROL
-------------------------------------
  SLICE04's CASE-1 recorded:
    REJECT_STAGE_LABELS the witness carries 3 generations, expected 4
  which is a ROW-COUNT rejection, not a substitution rejection.

  SLICE05's NC-P01 records:
    G3_BINDING_MISMATCH g3 recorded binding [STAGE1_POLYC] but the
    expected binding is [STAGE3_POLYC]
  which is the G3-SPECIFIC substitution rejection.  The SLICE04
  deficiency is CORRECTED, not repeated.

  NON_TARGET_FIELDS_UNCHANGED=YES
