THE FRESH-TREE BUILD
====================

THE BUILD WAS FROM A PRISTINE MATERIALISED TREE
---------------------------------------
COMMAND=git archive HEAD | tar -x -C /tmp/slice05-fresh
  The tree was materialised from the committed sources, then the C2
  production changes and the five qualified subject objects were
  copied in.  The compiler was then compiled FROM SOURCE there, in
  its own CMake build tree, with its own object files.

COMMAND=cd /tmp/slice05-fresh && make production-hcc
BUILD_EXIT_CODE=0
  PRODUCTION_PADDING_AUTHORITY=POLYC
  PRODUCTION_AST_BINOP_AUTHORITY=POLYC

  No object from the warm tree was reused for the COMPILER.  (The five
  qualified PolyC SUBJECT objects are inputs to the link by design,
  and their hashes are the ones the C2 commit recorded.)
