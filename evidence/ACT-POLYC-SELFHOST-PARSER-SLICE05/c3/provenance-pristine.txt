    g0 COMPILER_VERSION=Apple clang version 15.0.0 (clang-1500.0.40.1)

  THE FOUR COMPILERS ARE DISTINCT:
    g1 = ./hcc               (hcc v0.0.15-beta)

    g3 = ./build/hcc-bootstrap03 (hcc v0.0.15-beta)
    g1_G2_COMPILER_DISTINCT=YES
    g1_G3_COMPILER_DISTINCT=YES
    g2_G3_COMPILER_DISTINCT=YES
