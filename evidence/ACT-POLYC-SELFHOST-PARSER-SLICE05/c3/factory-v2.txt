THE FACTORY v2 RANGE CHECK
===========================

COMMAND=sh scripts/quality/factory-v2-range-check.sh \
          ACT-POLYC-SELFHOST-PARSER-SLICE05 HEAD

    STATUS=FAIL
    REASON=CLOSE 39d58c807eb72eccbc900e8ebe95f9f8293716db does not carry
           exactly one ACT-Phase: CLOSE (count=0)

  THIS IS THE EXPECTED AND CORRECT BEHAVIOUR AT C3.  The check
  requires the ACT's CLOSE commit, and by construction the CLOSE
  commit does not exist until C4.  The check is doing its job: it
  refuses to certify a range that has not been closed.

  The SAME CHECK IS RE-RUN AFTER THE C4 COMMIT IS CREATED, and its
  result is recorded in the CLOSE phase (see the external terminality
  section of the HANDOFF).  The check's PASS is NOT anticipated here.
