THE PRODUCTION AUTHORITY AT THE ENTRY HEAD
===========================================

The production compiler at the entry HEAD links these PolyC components.
Each authority is established by the SYMBOL in the LINKED PRODUCTION
BINARY, not by a grep of the source tree.

THE ENTRY-TREE PRODUCTION BUILD
------------------------------
BUILD=/tmp/slice05-baseline/build (configured from the ENTRY tree)

NOTE: the isolated entry-tree build above was configured with the four
self-hosted parser selectors OFF, because the entry tree's CMake
requires the prebuilt subject objects as inputs.  The authority below is
therefore measured on the REAL production build of the working tree, which
has every selector ON.
