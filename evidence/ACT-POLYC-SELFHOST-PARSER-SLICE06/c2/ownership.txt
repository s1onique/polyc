THE ENCLOSING FUNCTION IS NOT MIGRATED
---------------------------------------
parseCaseLabel as a WHOLE FUNCTION remains INELIGIBLE, exactly as the
predecessor's frozen contract says.  It still owns the host case list,
the arm statement list, the diagnostics and its subordinate host calls.
Only the ONE `if` condition changed.

The C side reads EXACTLY ONE host field, copies it as an I64, passes it
to the PolyC component, receives ONE I64 back, and acts on it by
executing the `break` itself.  NO other host graph field, callback,
pointer, diagnostic or recovery state crosses the ABI.

THE DECISION MAKES NO HOST-GRAPH WRITE, NO ALLOCATION, NO CALLBACK,
NO RECOVERY TRANSFER AND NO DIAGNOSTIC.
