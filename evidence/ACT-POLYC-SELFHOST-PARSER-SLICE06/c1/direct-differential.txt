THREE SOURCES ARE COMPARED ON EVERY ROW
------------------------------------------

   1. the legacy C oracle's observed decision (the REAL
      production src/parser.c object, delegation OFF);
   2. the PolyC component's observed decision;
   3. the fixture's own independently derived expected value.

Comparing only 1 against 2 would let an equally wrong fixture
hide a disagreement.  Comparing only 2 against 3 would let a
broken oracle hide a disagreement.  All three are compared.
