THE WITNESS IS INDEPENDENT OF THE PRODUCER
------------------------------------------

The producer uses a TRANSCRIBED enum table and a single emitter.
The witness uses a DIFFERENT path entirely: it READS THE LIVE
SOURCE TEXT of src/ast.h and src/parser.c and derives the
expectations by PARSING IT:

  DERIVED_ENUM_VALUES     = 41   (parsed out of the live src/ast.h)
  DERIVED_TERMINATING     = 6    (parsed out of the live
                                src/parser.c condition)
  DERIVED_TERMINATING_LINE= 1551 (the live condition line)
  DERIVED_TERMINATING_SET = 274 287 293 280 270 258
  DERIVED_PROBES          = 10

The witness therefore DETECTS a transcription error in the
producer: the two would disagree, and the wrong_expected counter
would move.
