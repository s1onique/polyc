WHY THE LEXER IS CONSERVED
---------------------------
The lexer is NOT touched by this ACT.  The switch-arm termination
decision is a PARSER decision: it is made after parseStatement has
returned, on the AstKind of the statement that was just parsed.  The
lexer produces the token stream; it never sees an AstKind.

  NO_CHANGE_TO=src/lexer.h
