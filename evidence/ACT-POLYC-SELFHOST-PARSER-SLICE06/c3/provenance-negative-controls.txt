THE PROVENANCE NEGATIVE CONTROLS
=================================

The provenance verifier is the PolyC tool
tools/quality/parser-slice06-provenance-verifier.HC.  It checks, for
EVERY generation and WITHOUT short-circuiting:

  * that the generation is present;
  * that its GENERATION_BINDING matches the EXPECTED binding for its
    stage;
  * that its RUN_BINDING matches too;
  * that its EXECUTION RECEIPT is present and carries its NONCE;
  * that its PROBE OUTPUT is present;
  * that its recorded COMPILER, OBJECT and SOURCE identities equal the
    LIVE identities of the named files;
  * that the four nonces are DISTINCT.

The NO-SHORT-CIRCUIT rule is the point: a control that breaks several
things at once cannot be satisfied by the first unrelated failure, and
every diagnostic is reported.

THE CONTROLS
-------------
Each control MUTATES the witness and re-runs the verifier.  A control
counts only when the INTENDED detector fires.

  NC-01  the G3 slot carries the G1 payload (a SUBSTITUTION)
         INTENDED: G3_BINDING_MISMATCH
         OBSERVED: SLICE06_PROV_BINDING_MISMATCH=g3
                   SLICE06_PROV_RECEIPT_MISMATCH=g3
                   SLICE06_PROV_DUPLICATE_NONCE=g0,g3
                   SLICE06_PROV_STATUS=FAIL
         SENSITIVE=YES

  NC-02  the G3 execution receipt is REMOVED (an OMISSION)
         INTENDED: G3_EXECUTION_RECEIPT_MISSING
         OBSERVED: SLICE06_PROV_MISSING_RECEIPT=g3
                   SLICE06_PROV_STATUS=FAIL
         SENSITIVE=YES

  NC-03  the recorded COMPILER identity is wrong
         INTENDED: COMPILER_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
         OBSERVED: SLICE06_PROV_COMPILER_IDENTITY_MISMATCH=g1
                   SLICE06_PROV_STATUS=FAIL
         SENSITIVE=YES

  NC-04  the recorded OBJECT identity is wrong
         INTENDED: OBJECT_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
         OBSERVED: SLICE06_PROV_OBJECT_IDENTITY_MISMATCH=g1
                   SLICE06_PROV_STATUS=FAIL
         SENSITIVE=YES

  NC-05  the STAGE is swapped (G2 carries the G3 binding)
         INTENDED: STAGE_IDENTITY_MISMATCH
         OBSERVED: SLICE06_PROV_BINDING_MISMATCH=g2
                   SLICE06_PROV_STATUS=FAIL
         SENSITIVE=YES

THE OBSERVER IS SEPARATE FROM THE PRODUCER
------------------------------------------
The verifier is a SEPARATE PolyC program from the Makefile rules that
PRODUCE the generations.  It reads the witness file that the producer
side wrote and it re-derives every identity by hashing the LIVE files
itself; it does not trust the recorded digests.  A producer that
recorded a digest which did not match the live file would be caught.

  OBSERVER_IS_SEPARATE_FROM_PRODUCER=YES
  OBSERVER_REHASHES_THE_LIVE_FILES=YES
  OBSERVER_TRUSTS_RECORDED_DIGESTS=NO
