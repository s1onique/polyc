THE DIRECTION
-------------
The pristine output has NO ARM1_MARKER: the compound statement
TERMINATES the first arm, so the marker statement is parsed by the
`case 2:` arm and is never printed.

The mutated output HAS ARM1_MARKER, and it comes FIRST: the compound
statement does NOT terminate the arm, so the marker statement stays in
the `case 1:` arm and IS printed.

That is EXACTLY the direction the predecessor's frozen causal contract
records, and it is the direction this ACT's PolyC mutation produces.

  PRISTINE_OUTPUT=SUB001:ARM2_MARKER,SUB001:x=1

WHAT THIS PROVES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE
--------------------------------------------
  PROVES=the switch-arm termination decision is OBSERVABLE in compiled
         program output, and that MUTATING THE POLYC DECISION changes
         that output, and that a byte-identical restoration returns the
         pristine output
  PROVES=the production delegation is LOAD-BEARING: the PolyC
         component's bytes are in the production compiler, and changing
         them changes the compiler's behaviour
  DOES_NOT_PROVE=that the parser is otherwise unchanged -- that is the
         conservation evidence's job (c3/parser-conservation.txt)
