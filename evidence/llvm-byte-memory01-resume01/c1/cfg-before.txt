=== Trigger condition ===

A function-local L is the function's return value, and:
  - L is conditionally modified inside one arm of a
    conditional branch (single conditional or nested diamond),
  - the exit_block has multiple predecessors,
  - not every predecessor defines L.

When irForwardReturnSlot rewrites the exit_block's
`store slot, L; load slot; ret load_result` into
`ret L`, the rewrite is unsafe because L is not defined on
every predecessor path.

=== Width classification ===

- I8 (U8) access: the defect reproduces with byte-typed locals
  (ReadDigit, AccDigit).
- I64 access: the defect reproduces with I64-typed locals
  (i64_collapse_probe.HC, single_cond_probe.HC).
- F64 access: not tested; if the same idiom applies to F64
  returns, the defect is also present for floats. Out of scope
  for RESUME01 but worth noting for follow-on ACTs.
