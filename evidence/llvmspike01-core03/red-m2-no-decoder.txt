RED-M2 — harness has no per-fixture matrix decoder
===================================================

scripts/quality/llvm-spike-test.sh currently runs each fixture
and checks:

  positive fixture:  hcc --emit-llvm rc=0, .ll non-empty,
                     llvm-as rc=0, no alloca/store/load ops.
  negative fixture:  hcc --emit-llvm rc!=0, expected diagnostic
                     string in stderr.

But there is no per-fixture decoder that prints:

  expected_class:    <SUPPORTED | REJECTED | ...>
  observed_class:    <...>
  expected_diagnostic: <LLVM_BACKEND_UNSUPPORTED_*>
  observed_diagnostic: <from stderr>
  defensive_trips:   <count>

CORE03 M3 adds this decoder.

CLOSURE PROOF for M2:
  - For each of the 18 fixtures, the harness prints a
    `=== contract check: <fixture> ===` block.
  - The block has 5 fields as above.
  - All 18 blocks PASS.
