Provenance measurement for the bootstrap hcc that produces
the canonical libtos.a archive.

BOOTSTRAP_BINARY:        build/hcc-bootstrap04
BOOTSTRAP_SIZE_BYTES:    751760
BOOTSTRAP_SHA256:        3b4474213efabcda30eb2d0c0610c6e23793f876896c8e21c5e18024b1ab40f6
BOOTSTRAP_VERSION:       hcc - HolyC Compiler v0.0.15-beta

The binary at the time of CORRECTION02 C2 entry is the
immutable producer. Any future rebuild that uses a binary
with a different SHA256 (or different size) is a different
producer and the archive's producer-id note will not match.

Reproduction command:
  shasum -a 256 build/hcc-bootstrap04 | cut -d' ' -f1

This SHA will be:
  - embedded in the lib-tos precondition error message (AC03)
  - baked into the libtos.a producer-id note (AC04)
