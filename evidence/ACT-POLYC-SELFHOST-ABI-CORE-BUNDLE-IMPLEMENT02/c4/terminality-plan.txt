BLOCKING_DISCREPANCY_1=C0-P1. The frozen contract's section 2 table pairs each of the three member source digests with a byte range that does NOT hash to that digest under ANY of the tested conventions, and an exhaustive search over the whole committed src/aarch64.c blob finds NO interval with those digests. The digests belong to the predecessor's C1 record's DIFFERENT byte sets (583, 442 and 152 bytes) rather than to the C2 contract's (1059, 918 and 153 bytes).
BLOCKING_DISCREPANCY_2=C0-P2. The frozen ABI's third export, U0 AbiA64CmpCcEmitCSet(I64, I64), has no observable output channel: its parameter list carries two copied scalars, no pointer, no callback and no state handle; its return type carries no value; and the frozen contract forbids a callback, a global write, an assembly-buffer pointer and turning the return into a suffix pointer. The decision it must carry (which suffix the cset materialises) therefore cannot be transported.
