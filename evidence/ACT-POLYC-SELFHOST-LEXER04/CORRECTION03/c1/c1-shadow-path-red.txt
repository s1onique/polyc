Expected outcome after fix: MUTATED_SUBJECT_DIVERGENCE_DETECTED=YES.

Known broken target: lib-complex_1.0 -> lib-complex_1co.
Source of the corruption: legacy token-by-token concatenation at
src/lexer.c:1993 (aoStrCatPrintf %.*s with next.len / next.start).
When the angle form is "#link <lib-complex_1.0>", the lexer tokenizes
the body as a sequence of tokens. The current Lexer punts on '.'
inside a TK_IDENT (it lexes "." as TK_PUNCT) so the body becomes
"lib-complex_1" + "." + "0", concatenated byte-by-byte with no
preservation of the original ASCII character run. The PolyC component
sees each token independently and is therefore NOT responsible for the
defect -- the legacy assembly is.

After C2 IMPL: BootstrapLinkDirective will be called once with the
unmodified directive body bytes, and out_stored_len / out_consumed
will drive AoStr assembly. The corruption disappears by construction.
