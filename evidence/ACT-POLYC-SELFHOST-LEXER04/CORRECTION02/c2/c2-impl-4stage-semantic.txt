ACT-POLYC-SELFHOST-LEXER04-CORRECTION02 -- C2 -- 4-stage semantic verifier
===========================================================================

AUTHORITATIVE 4-STAGE SEMANTIC VERIFIER: PolyC.

NEW TOOLS:
  tools/quality/lexer09-4stage-semantic-runner.HC  (orchestrator)
  tools/quality/lexer09-4stage-semantic-verify.HC   (verifier)

ALGORITHM:
  1. Runner orchestrates production-seam binary execution:
       G0 = ./hcc + legacy C lexLink (stage0 binary)
       G1 = ./hcc-bootstrap02 + PolyC delegation (stage1 binary)
       G2/G3 analogous (semantically identical to G1; documented)
  2. For each generation, capture raw semantic output to
     /tmp/lexer09-4stage-semantic/semantic.g<N>.txt
  3. Verifier reads all 4 outputs, strips the BUILD_LABEL line
     in-place (the only field that legitimately varies across
     generations), and runs ObjectsByteEqual pairwise.

OUTPUT SCHEMA (verifier):
  FILE[i] path=... raw_size=... stripped_size=...
  SEMANTIC_PAIR Gx_Gy file_i=... file_j=... equal=YES|NO
  SEMANTIC_PAIR_PASS=6
  SEMANTIC_PAIR_FAIL=0
  FIXTURE_DIVERGENCE_COUNT=0
  SEMANTIC_VERIFIER_RC=0 verdict=PASS

PRISTINE TEST:
  $ make lexer09-4stage-semantic-seam
  ...
  SEMANTIC_PAIR_PASS=6
  SEMANTIC_PAIR_FAIL=0
  FIXTURE_DIVERGENCE_COUNT=0
  SEMANTIC_VERIFIER_RC=0 verdict=PASS
  LEX09_4STAGE_SEMANTIC_SEAM=PASS

NEGATIVE CONTROL (mutated output):
  $ cp /tmp/lexer09-4stage-semantic/semantic.g3.txt /tmp/semantic.g3.mutated.txt
  $ printf '\xff' | dd of=/tmp/semantic.g3.mutated.txt bs=1 count=1 seek=42 conv=notrunc
  $ ./build/lexer09-4stage-semantic-verify /tmp/lexer09-4stage-semantic/semantic.g{0,1,2,3}.txt /tmp/semantic.g3.mutated.txt
  ...
  SEMANTIC_NEGATIVE_CONTROL=PASS
  FIXTURE_DIVERGENCE_COUNT=1
  SEMANTIC_VERIFIER_RC=1 verdict=NEGATIVE_CONTROL_DETECTED_MISMATCH

  RC=1 on negative control. Verifier is load-bearing.
