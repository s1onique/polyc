PRE-EXISTING SEMANTIC NOTE:

  L07 (angle form with '.0' suffix) demonstrates a legacy lexer
  pre-existing quirk: the '1.0' gets split into '1' and '.0' by
  the lexer (the '.' is treated as part of a number). The C-side
  wrapper concatenates both tokens to 'lib-complex_10' (or similar
  with the TK_FLOAT lexeme). This is NOT a LEXER04 regression; it
  is the legacy behavior of the production parser, and is reproduced
  byte-identically at stage0 and stage1. The C3 contract is
  semantic equivalence, not new behavior.
