# D4 -- THE SIGNED INTEGER PARSER OVERFLOWS
=======================================================================

The parser under test is the VERBATIM parse_int of
tools/quality/parser-control-flow-token-differential.HC. The probe below
extracts that function MECHANICALLY (sed -n 48,86p) -- it is not a
hand-written paraphrase -- and calls it directly.

PROBE_SOURCE=build/harden01/int/d4probe.HC
PROBE_EXTRACTION=sed -n 48,86p tools/quality/parser-control-flow-token-differential.HC
PROBE_BINARY=build/harden01/int/d4probe.bin
PROBE_RUN_COMMAND=./build/harden01/int/d4probe.bin
PROBE_OUTPUT:
  CASE=INT64_MAX input=9223372036854775807
    rc=0 value=-1
  CASE=INT64_MIN input=-9223372036854775808
    rc=0 value=0
  CASE=POSITIVE_OVERFLOW input=9223372036854775808
    rc=0 value=0
  CASE=NEGATIVE_OVERFLOW input=-9223372036854775809
    rc=0 value=-1
  CASE=HEX_MAX input=0x7fffffffffffffff
    rc=0 value=-1
  CASE=HEX_MIN input=-0x8000000000000000
    rc=0 value=0
  CASE=HEX_OVERFLOW input=0x8000000000000000
    rc=0 value=0
  CASE=TRAILING_GARBAGE input=123abc
    rc=2 value=123
  CASE=NO_DIGITS input=abc
    rc=1 value=123

Reading the output:
  * INT64_MAX  9223372036854775807  -> value=-1   (WRONG: must be 9223372036854775807)
  * INT64_MIN -9223372036854775808  -> value=0    (WRONG: must be -9223372036854775808)
  * 9223372036854775808            -> rc=0       (WRONG: out of range must be REJECTED)
  * -9223372036854775809           -> rc=0       (WRONG: out of range must be REJECTED)
  * 0x7fffffffffffffff             -> value=-1   (WRONG: must be 9223372036854775807)
  * 0x8000000000000000             -> rc=0       (WRONG: out of range must be REJECTED)
  * 123abc                         -> rc=2       (OK: trailing garbage detected by the CALLER)
  * abc                            -> rc=1       (OK: no digits)

The cause is the signed accumulator: v = v*10 + digit overflows the
I64 range before the negation, so the value silently wraps and an
out-of-range magnitude is ACCEPTED rather than rejected.

D4_SIGNED_OVERFLOW_WRAPS=YES   <-- RED
D4_OUT_OF_RANGE_ACCEPTED=YES   <-- RED
D4_EXPECTED=checked accumulation with an unsigned magnitude; reject out-of-range
