# ---- WHY THE MAGNITUDE IS ACCUMULATED IN THE NEGATIVE DOMAIN ---
# This was MEASURED, not assumed. In this PolyC:
#   * a U64 literal at or above 2^63 is parsed wrong: the literal
#     9223372036854775808 evaluates to 7fffffffffffffff (the lexer
#     saturates at I64_MAX), while (1 << 63) is correct;
#   * U64 shift-right, division and comparison SIGN-EXTEND at 2^63;
#   * U64 multiply/add WRAP in the signed domain: accumulating
#     9223372036854775808 into a U64 yields efd39c2bce007000.
# The signed-negative domain is the only one in which the arithmetic
# is exact, and it makes the magnitude 2^63 (needed by INT64_MIN)
# representable.
UNSIGNED_ACCUMULATION_REJECTED_BECAUSE_MEASURED_UNSOUND=YES
