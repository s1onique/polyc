## Range-checker is CLOSE-only by design

`factory-v2-range-check.sh` validates the FULL range from FIRST to
CLOSE.  At C3, HEAD = C2 (no CLOSE exists yet).  Running the range
checker against HEAD reports:

```
$ bash scripts/quality/factory-v2-range-check.sh \
    ACT-POLYC-SELFHOST-PARSER-PADDING01-CORRECTION04 HEAD
STATUS=FAIL
REASON=CLOSE 53bca9d3 does not carry exactly one ACT-Phase: CLOSE
```

This is the EXPECTED behavior of the range-checker; it is not a
defect.  The C3 phase verifies the trailer contract for the
already-committed commits independently.
