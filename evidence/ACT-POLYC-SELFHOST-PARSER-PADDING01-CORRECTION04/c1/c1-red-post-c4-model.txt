## The contradiction

A C4 commit exists at time T.  After T, additional commits MAY
or MAY NOT be created.  Only an observation AFTER T can confirm
POST_C4_COMMIT_COUNT = 0.

Any attempt to commit that observation creates a new commit, which
contradicts the value it claims to record.

CORRECTION03 used:
  git rev-list --count HEAD..HEAD = 0
which is true at every commit (an empty range trivially yields 0).
It is not a load-bearing post-C4 external witness.
