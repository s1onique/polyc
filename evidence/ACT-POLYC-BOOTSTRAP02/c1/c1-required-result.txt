Notes on the B0-vs-production grammar discrepancy
=================================================

The discrepancy is bounded and required. The production
source tree itself uses `$`-bearing identifiers (e.g.
_opendir$INODE64 in src/holyc-lib/dir.HC), so the B1
component MUST accept `$` in continuation to be
production-equivalent. B0 grammar forbids `$`, but B0 is
preserved unchanged per §12.

Per C1-CORRECTION01, the original §5 halt predicate
("production grammar broader or narrower than B0") was
replaced by the corrected three-clause predicate
("production cannot be frozen OR B1 cannot preserve
production OR satisfying production requires changing B0").
None of the three corrected clauses fires.

The discrepancy is documented in:
  - c1/identifier-contract.txt    (full grammar comparison)
  - c1/legacy-ident-oracle.tsv    (I15 fixture exercises
                                   "$ in continuation")
  - c1/c1-required-result.txt     (this file; AC03 result)

If a future ACT narrows B1 grammar to B0 grammar, that
ACT must first re-prove that production sources do not
require `$` in continuation; otherwise that ACT would
trigger HALT_B1_SEMANTIC_MISMATCH at C3.

Notes on the bounded ctype claim
================================

Per C1-CORRECTION01, B1's contract is explicitly
ASCII-only. The B1 component (bootstrap02-ident.HC) must
use explicit byte comparisons and MUST NOT call
isalpha/isalnum. Non-ASCII production behaviour
(bytes >= 0x80) is locale-sensitive and possibly
undefined on signed-char platforms; B1 does not claim
equivalence. See evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02-C1-CORRECTION01/c1/
corrected-ctype-claim.txt for the bounded ctype claim.

Notes on the reference-model oracle
===================================

Per C1-CORRECTION01, the standalone C99 oracle at
tools/quality/bootstrap02-ident-oracle.c is re-classified
as EVIDENTIARY_ROLE = REFERENCE_MODEL. It is not a verbatim
transcription of the production scanner; it adapts the
Lexer-state cursor to (src, src_len, start, out_end) and
normalizes ctype through unsigned char. It is mechanically
faithful for the ASCII fixture corpus, but C3 must
additionally test the stage0/stage1 compiler corpus
(e.g. src/holyc-lib/dir.HC) to validate production
equivalence. See evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02-C1-CORRECTION01/c1/
corrected-oracle-role.txt.
