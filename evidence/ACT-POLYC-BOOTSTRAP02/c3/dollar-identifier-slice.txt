Section A — Enumeration command
------------------------------------------------------------

  grep -R -l -E '[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*\$[A-Za-z0-9_$]*' \
       --include='*.HC' \
       src/holyc-lib src/tests | LC_ALL=C sort

This finds identifiers (matching [A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*)
followed by `$` and a continuation suffix
([A-Za-z0-9_$]*). The `$` is the byte discovered during
the C1 review of the identifier grammar.
