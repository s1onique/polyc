Finding A (asserted P0): "C3 committed generated
binaries despite the C3 contract forbidding them"
------------------------------------------------------------

Reviewer-asserted contract clause:
    "No binaries. No object files. No generated
     compiler committed."

The active ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02 and its predecessor
corrections (C1-CORRECTION01, C2-CORRECTION01..03)
contain NO such clause. A repository-wide grep across
`docs/`, `evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02/`, and
`docs/factory/` finds zero textual source for this
exact wording.

The closest documented precedent is
ACT-POLYC-LLVM-SPIKE01-RESUME01-CORRECTION01 §4
("No binary artefacts committed under
evidence/llvmspike01-resume01-correction01/"),
which is scoped to that specific ACT and is not
binding here.

Mechanical disposition:

  - The substantive engineering concern is valid:
    committing ~2.9 MB of Mach-O executables that add
    no information beyond the textual SHA-256 records
    already in `reproducibility.txt` is wasteful
    payload and a poor evidence-shape choice.
  - The cost of deletion is negligible; the cleanliness
    benefit is real. The four committed files are
    forward-deleted in this commit:
      evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02/c3/rebuild-A/hcc
      evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02/c3/rebuild-A/hcc-bootstrap02
      evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02/c3/rebuild-B/hcc
      evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02/c3/rebuild-B/hcc-bootstrap02
  - The empty `rebuild-A/` and `rebuild-B/`
    directories are removed.
  - The textual SHA-256 records in `reproducibility.txt`
    are unchanged. The build commands (Section A and
    Section B) remain canonical.

Finding B (asserted P1): "unit-test / jit-unit-test
wording is too strong"
------------------------------------------------------------

The C3 EVIDENCE binding result on `de6b30c` contained:

    "unit-test/jit-unit-test INHERITED 90/90"

This wording can be misread as a current PASS. The
mechanically truthful status is:

    UNIT_TEST_CURRENT_EXECUTION =
      ENVIRONMENTALLY_UNAVAILABLE / RUNNER_FALSE_GREEN
    UNIT_TEST_LAST_KNOWN_GOOD    = 90/90
    JIT_UNIT_TEST_CURRENT_EXECUTION =
      ENVIRONMENTALLY_UNAVAILABLE / RUNNER_FALSE_GREEN
    JIT_UNIT_TEST_LAST_KNOWN_GOOD    = 90/90
    C3_ATTRIBUTABLE_REGRESSION    = NONE_OBSERVED

Two distinct facts are kept separate:

  1. the environment/install-path problem is inherited
     from the C1 entry baseline
     (/usr/local/include/tos.HH absent);
  2. the current `make unit-test` / `make jit-unit-test`
     recipe uses `&&` and does not propagate child
     compilation failures to make's rc. This is a
     Makefile-level test-infrastructure defect
     (RUNNER_FALSE_GREEN), NOT a B1 compiler defect.

This wording fix is applied to:
  - evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02/c3/compiler-conservation.txt
      (Section C and Section D)
  - evidence/ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02/c3/c3-required-result.txt
      (Section F)
