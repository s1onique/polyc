------------------------------------------------------------
Delegation binding (lexIdentifier -> BootstrapScanIdent)
------------------------------------------------------------

Per C3 delegation-witness, stage1's lexIdentifier static
delegation routes to BootstrapScanIdent via two `bl`
instructions. Confirmed on the freshly rebuilt stage1:

```text
$ otool -tv build/hcc-bootstrap02 | grep -B1 'BootstrapScanIdent'
00000001000303f0	mov	x2, #0x0
00000001000303f4	bl	_BootstrapScanIdent
--
000000010003145c	mov	x2, #0x0
0000000100031460	bl	_BootstrapScanIdent
--
000000010007c314	ret
_BootstrapScanIdent:
```

Two `bl _BootstrapScanIdent` instruction sites. Both
land inside stage1's linked image at addresses in the
production code segment, not the B1 component object
(loaded separately at link time and resolved by the
static linker).
