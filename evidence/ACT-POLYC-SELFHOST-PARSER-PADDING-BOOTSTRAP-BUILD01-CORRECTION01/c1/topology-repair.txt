    01c0822  C0  ACT-Phase: RED        <- FIRST (rule 4)

  predecessor's id does not continue past the predecessor's CLOSE

  (rule 5 for this ACT).

  The external terminality observations that the predecessor recorded in
  its post-CLOSE commit are recorded HERE, INSIDE the range, in C3. The
  content is preserved; only its position changes.

WHY A CORRECTION ACT RATHER THAN A REWRITE

  predecessor's range cannot be rewritten. The Factory's correction
  mechanism (GIT-METADATA.md section 2.3) is the established remedy for
  exactly this situation: a NEW act id, ACT-Supersedes pointing at the
  predecessor, and ACT-Corrected-Verdict recording the predecessor's
  corrected verdict.

  The predecessor's own HANDOFF and evidence are NOT rewritten (F14);
  the corrected verdict is recorded in THIS ACT's artifacts.
