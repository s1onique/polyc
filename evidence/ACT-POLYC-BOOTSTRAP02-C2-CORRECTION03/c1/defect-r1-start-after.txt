ACT-POLYC-BOOTSTRAP02-C2-CORRECTION03 — C1 RED —
defect P2 (folded from R1 of CORRECTION02):
l->start_after divergence
==================================================

The real-Lexer seam witness established this
mechanically:

  legacy:    l->start_after = l->ptr_after
            (set by the LAST lexNextChar in the
             legacy identifier loop)

  stage1:    l->start_after = l->start_at_pre_call
            (the B1 path never sets l->start;
             src/lexer.c:911-939 only sets
             l->cur_strlen and l->ptr)

Re-reviewer verdict (CORRECTION02 re-review):

    "If that data-flow claim holds across all users,
     this is an internal invariant mismatch with no
     current observable consequence."

The grep audit (searching for `l->start` reads
after lexIdentifier in src/lexer.c lines 960-1557,
src/parser.c, src/cctrl.c, src/ast.c,
src/compile.c) shows:

  - The only read of `l->start` is at
    src/lexer.c:1209 (`start = l->start`),
    which is BEFORE `ch = lexNextChar(l)` on the
    NEXT iteration. So `l->start` is captured
    fresh by the next `lexNextChar` overwriting it.

  - No consumer (parser, controller, AST builder,
    compile driver) reads `l->start` directly.

Severity: P2 / cleanup. BLOCKS_NEXT = NO.

However, the reviewer's recommended fix is
simpler than continuing to argue about
reachability:

    "Reproduce the legacy postcondition rather than
     arguing that no current consumer sees it. That
     costs almost nothing and removes an unnecessary
     state-invariant divergence."

This correction ACT folds the l->start fix into
the same bounded production-source change as the
EOF fix. The combined fix is two lines.
