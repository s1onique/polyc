--- WHY THE BASELINE WAS NOT UPDATED ---
The baseline (evidence/ACT-POLYC-TOOLING-SHELL-INVENTORY01/c1/baseline.txt)
is the FROZEN artifact of the closed ACT ACT-POLYC-TOOLING-SHELL-INVENTORY01.
F14 forbids mutating a closed ACT's evidence tree. Updating the baseline
from this ACT would be exactly that.

--- WHY THIS IS NOT A BLOCKER ---
1. scripts/quality/shell-loc-gate.sh is NOT wired into gate-fast or
   gate-push (verified by grep over the gate graph). It is a standalone
   ratchet.
2. The gate was already failing at the entry commit with 2 pre-existing
   violations (see BASELINE STATE above).
3. The ratchet's PURPOSE is to push shell tooling toward PolyC. The
   Factory-v2 validators are grandfathered LEGACY tools (recorded in
   docs/factory/LEGACY-NON-POLYC-TOOLS.tsv as pre-SHELL-INVENTORY01), and
   this ACT is explicitly authorized to maintain them:
     "Existing shell validator maintenance is permitted"
   (ACT section 5, OUT OF SCOPE note).
4. The growth is bounded and mechanical:
     factory-v2-commit-msg-check.sh  218 -> 344  (+126)
     factory-v2-range-check.sh       249 -> 333  (+84)
     factory-v2-test.sh              465 -> 831  (+366)
   The shared normalization was deliberately factored into ONE sourced
   library (scripts/quality/factory-v2-trailer-compat.inc, 119 LOC) so
   that the two validators do not each carry a second copy of the
   trailer grammar. That library is a sourced include, not an executable
   tool, and is therefore outside the "new shell tool" rule of
   F-POLYC-TOOLS section 27.

--- RESIDUE OWNER ---
The proper long-term resolution is to migrate the Factory-v2 validators
to PolyC, which is downstream work under the shell-budget programme.
This ACT does not claim that work.
