CONCLUSION: the continuation detector only inspects direct children of CLOSE.
An intervening ordinary commit on the descendant path hides a same-ACT
continuation. This is a SEPARATE defect from the trailer-parse defect,
and it is INSIDE this ACT scope (ACT section 8 Work package C:
"Reject a same-ACT continuation after CLOSE, including cases with
intervening ordinary commits on a relevant descendant path").
