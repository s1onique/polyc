RATIONALE
=========

The bounded I64 spike is authorised to be SSA-only. Multiple reaching
stores require either (a) phi nodes at every join block, or (b)
rejection of the shape. The previous IMPL chose (a) silently by
emitting nothing for the second store and letting the last-encountered
binding survive — which is wrong. This IMPL chooses (b) explicitly
by rejecting any second IR_STORE.

A "second IR_STORE" is, by construction, a multi-def. There is no
need for dominance analysis: if two IR_STOREs target the same local
id, the local has at least two reaching definitions and therefore
must be either phi'd or rejected. The spike rejects.

This matches the ACT's contract: "no PHI, no alloca/store/load
fallback". The shape is closed off at the IR_STORE boundary; the
test fixture makes that visible.
