Harness redirection (P0-2 second half):
---------------------------------------

The previous IMPL's harness script regenerates C1 evidence files
on every run. This is wrong: C1 evidence is historical. The fix
redirects the multi_def RED output to this ACT's evidence dir
(evidence/llvmspike01-resume01-correction01-resume01-correction01/
red-multi_def/) and not into C1.

The 12-fixture matrix harness remains intact (it writes to
$REPO_ROOT/evidence/llvmspike01-resume01/, the predecessor spike's
matrix evidence dir, which is the *active* matrix, not a historical
RED). That dir is the ACT's own evidence and is updated freely.

The new multi_def fixture is registered in a SEPARATE invocation
section of the harness that writes ONLY into this ACT's
correction01-resume01-correction01/ evidence dir.
