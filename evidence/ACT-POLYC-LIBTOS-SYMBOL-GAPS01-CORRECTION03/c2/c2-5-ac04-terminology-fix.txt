C2-5: AC04 terminology fix (closes RED-5).

The CORRECTION02 c3-ac04.txt framed producer_id.o as "under __.SYMDEF",
which is technically wrong: __.SYMDEF is the BSD/Darwin archive symbol
table, not a containing directory.

The C0 ACT (this ACT) explicitly authorizes the following framing
amendment (C0 §3.5):

  AC04 evidence says: `ar t libtos.a` lists __.SYMDEF (symbol table),
  all.o, errno_shim.o, and producer_id.o as four SIBLING members.
  nm -j producer_id.o reports __producer_id as a defined symbol.
  strings producer_id.o | grep -qF <expected-SHA> confirms the SHA-256
  is baked in.

NO PRODUCTION MUTATION REQUIRED for this defect.

VERDICT: PASS (RED-5 closed by procedure amendment).
