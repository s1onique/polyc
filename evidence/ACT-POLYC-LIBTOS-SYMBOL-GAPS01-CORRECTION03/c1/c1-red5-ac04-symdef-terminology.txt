RED-5: AC04 evidence used incorrect framing. "Pristine archive
       has the expected producer identity. PASS iff `ar t
       build/test-prefix/lib/libtos.a` lists `__.SYMDEF` and
       the producer identity note under `__.SYMDEF` matches
       the bootstrap hcc commit."

       This framing is wrong: `__.SYMDEF` is the BSD/Darwin
       archive symbol table mapping SYMBOLS to MEMBERS, not a
       container in which members live. producer_id.o is a
       regular archive member alongside __.SYMDEF, all.o,
       errno_shim.o.

REVIEWER QUOTE:
  "One terminology correction: producer_id.o is a regular
   archive member; __.SYMDEF is the BSD/Darwin archive symbol
   table mapping symbols to members, not a container in
   which producer_id.o lives. LLVM's archive implementation
   makes that distinction explicitly."

PROCEDURE:
  cat .../CORRECTION02/c3/c3-ac04.txt
  ar t build/test-prefix/lib/libtos.a
  ar tv build/test-prefix/lib/libtos.a
