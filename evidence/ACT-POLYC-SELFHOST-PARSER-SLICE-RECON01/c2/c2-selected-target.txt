  Decide how many padding bytes (0..size-1) are required to align the
  next field at `offset` to a `size`-byte boundary, with a guard for
  zero-size fields (e.g. trailing `U0 body;` placeholders) that need
  no padding.

  I64  size        # candidate size; >=0 (0 means "no padding needed")
