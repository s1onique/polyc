    The fixture lowers Max(a, b) with diamond CFG. Param copies a and
    b are pre-allocated to stack slots via llPreallocateLocals at
    src/llvm-backend.c:376-414. Each load reads back through the
    slot, even though SSA could express the params directly.

Sample emitted LLVM IR (04_cmp_branch.HC, verbatim from /tmp/lltmp/04_cmp_branch.ll):

    define i64 @Max(i64 %0, i64 %1) {
    bb1:
      %2 = alloca i64, align 8
      %3 = alloca i64, align 8
      store i64 %0, ptr %2, align 4
      store i64 %1, ptr %3, align 4
      %4 = icmp sgt i64 %0, %1
      br i1 %4, label %bb3, label %bb4

    bb3:                                              ; preds = %bb1
      %5 = load i64, ptr %2, align 4
      ret i64 %5

    bb4:                                              ; preds = %bb1
      %6 = load i64, ptr %3, align 4
      ret i64 %6
    ...

Note: the icmp itself correctly uses %0/%1 directly (NOT through
allocas), so the predicate mapping is fine. The waste is on the
ret-value path: bb3 loads %2 instead of returning %0; bb4 loads %3
instead of returning %1.

This is exactly the "PARAM_COPY" pattern called out in ACT §24.
