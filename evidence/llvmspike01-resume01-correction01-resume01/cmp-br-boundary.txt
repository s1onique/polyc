Fresh recon at HEAD:

    (produces IR_CMP_BR via a CMP+BR fusion)

Caller of irOptPinResultReg:
    irFunctionPrepForCodeGen (src/ir-regalloc.c)

        src/x86_64.c:2560 (via x86_64GenerateFunction)

        src/x86_64-jit.c:1583 (via jit path)
        src/aarch64-jit.c:1707 (via jit path)

      directly (src/llvm-backend.c:859)
