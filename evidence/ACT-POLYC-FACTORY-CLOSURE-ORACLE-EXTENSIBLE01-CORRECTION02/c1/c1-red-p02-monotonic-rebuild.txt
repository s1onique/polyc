  - The diff 1054ae2..HEAD is empty, in which case the SHA at
    HEAD == the SHA at 1054ae2 == 13c843e5ff..., and the

  - The SHA at C3 is c21289718c..., in which case the diff
    1054ae2..HEAD is non-empty, contradicting the claim of
    "manifest unchanged".
