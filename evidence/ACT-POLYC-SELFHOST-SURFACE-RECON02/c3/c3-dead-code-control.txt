ACT-POLYC-SELFHOST-SURFACE-RECON02 -- C3 dead/nonruntime exclusion control
==========================================================================

Control: a non-compiler-semantic surface (e.g. INV.UTIL.MISC, INV.MEM.ARENA,
         INV.JIT.X86, INV.LSP, INV.TRANSPILE) must NOT be classified as a
         migration candidate.

Method: read c1-blocker-classification.tsv for those surface_ids and
        confirm blocker_class in {DOES_NOT_BLOCK_SELFHOST} and
        candidate_for_next == 'NO'.

  INV.MEM.ARENA        blocker_class=DOES_NOT_BLOCK_SELFHOST  candidate=NO
