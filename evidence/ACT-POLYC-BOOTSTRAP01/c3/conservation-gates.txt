Why `make unit-test` cannot be re-run fresh in this
particular sandbox:

  The inherited `src/tests/run.HC` invokes its child
  compiles via `System("../../hcc %s", filename)`,
  where the top-level `./hcc` was built with the
  default `INSTALL_PREFIX=/usr/local`. In this sandbox
  `/usr/local/include/tos.HH` does not exist, so the
  inner compiles fail with "Failed to open file:
  /usr/local/include/tos.HH".

  This is a pre-existing environmental residue, NOT
  caused by B0:
    - At the pre-B0 commit (72f61e3) the SAME 67
      "Failed to compile" pattern occurs.
    - The predecessor ACT-POLYC-AOT-PIC-EXTERNAL-REFS01
      c3 ran the SAME gate on the SAME hcc (b76f0d8)
      and recorded `unit-test 90/90 PASS` in
      evidence/ACT-POLYC-AOT-PIC-EXTERNAL-REFS01/c3/
      unit-test.txt.
    - The /usr/local path mismatch is a host-level
      configuration gap, not a B0-caused regression.

  F6 (no silent fallback) is honoured: B0 does not
  pretend `make unit-test` passes here. B0 records the
  inherited PASS at the predecessor tree.
