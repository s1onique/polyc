  NOTE ON THE CLOSURE PRECONDITION LIST
  ACT section 40 lists "GATE_PUSH_UNMODIFIED=PASS" as a closure
  precondition. That precondition is NOT met, because a pre-existing
  defect fails the gate. ACT section 33 provides the governing rule:
  "If gate-push fails for a separate pre-existing defect, classify that
  defect with its own evidence. Do not claim full gate qualification."

  The C4 verdict therefore CANNOT be PASS_TRUE_GREEN: that verdict
  would assert a fully green gate. The honest verdict is a HALT that
  records the measured state and the pre-existing residue, with
  HALT_CLASS=GOVERNANCE / BLOCKS_NEXT=NO per the F-MECHANICAL-BLOCKING
  grammar -- the engineering objective of this ACT (a clean-checkout
  build that produces its own PolyC padding object) IS met and IS
  measured, but the full pre-push gate is not green for a reason that
  predates this ACT.
