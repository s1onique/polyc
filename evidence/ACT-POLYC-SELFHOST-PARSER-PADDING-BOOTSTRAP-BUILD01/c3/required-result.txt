  ACT section 40 lists "GATE_PUSH_UNMODIFIED=PASS" as a closure
  precondition. That precondition is NOT met, because a pre-existing
  defect fails the gate. ACT section 42 is explicit about the
  consequence: "If a binding mandatory predicate fails, use the
  appropriate honest HALT_* verdict and the Factory-required HALT_CLASS
  and BLOCKS_NEXT trailers."

  The C4 verdict is therefore HALT_GATE_PUSH_NOT_GREEN, with
  HALT_CLASS=GOVERNANCE and BLOCKS_NEXT=NO. It is NOT
  PASS_TRUE_GREEN, and it is not a PASS-with-residue either: the
  mechanical classifier (tools/factory/factory-halt-classification.HC)
  REJECTS HALT_CLASS on a PASS verdict, so a PASS token is not
  available. The verdict was validated against that classifier:

    MODE=ACT
    STATUS=PASS
    VERDICT=HALT_GATE_PUSH_NOT_GREEN
    HALT_CLASS=GOVERNANCE
    BLOCKS_NEXT=NO

  Every ENGINEERING predicate of ACT section 40 IS met and measured.
  The one unmet predicate is the full gate, and it is unmet for a
  reason that predates this ACT.
