Test 8: space-before-tab in initial indent of an added line.

Setup: a commit that adds a file whose first line begins with a
literal " " followed by a tab.

  $ git diff-tree --check --root -m --no-commit-id -r SP
  indent.txt:1: space before tab in indent.
  + \tindented with space-tab
  EXIT: 2

This is also part of the CORRECTION02 P1 fix. Git's --check
policy covers space-before-tab in addition to trailing
whitespace. The CORRECTION01 awk scanner happened to match this
case (the regex /^\+[ \t]+$/ catches a "+\t" line), but it was
incidental rather than principled.

With the diff-tree primitive, ALL of Git's documented --check
rules apply uniformly, including any future additions to
core.whitespace configuration. We do not reimplement Git's
whitespace policy; we call Git.
