==================================================

C2 IMPL artifacts added or modified in this commit:

NEW PolyC files (5):

  tools/quality/lexer07-sha256.HC          (173 LOC)
    PolyC-local SHA-256 (FIPS 180-4). Verified
    against NIST SHA-2 Additional Test Data:
    empty, "abc", 55/56/57-byte zero-byte
    padding-boundary cases. All 5 self-test vectors
    pass. The hcc compiler codegen has known quirks
    (no C-style casts; `dst[i*2]` indexing emits
    `strb w0, [x1, x2, lsl #1]` which the assembler
    rejects; bitwise-NOT on U8 fails pointer index);
    each quirk is documented and worked around.
    LOC exceeds the ACT budget (≤120) because a
    correct reference SHA-256 with NIST-vector
    self-tests cannot fit in 120 LOC without
    compromising readability or correctness; the
    over-budget is documented and recorded in C03
    EVIDENCE.

  tools/quality/lexer07-proof-verify.HC    (136 LOC)
    Independent PolyC verifier (AC02 + AC19 +
    AC20). Reads only on-disk evidence files
    (corpus-matrix.tsv, corpus-object-provenance.tsv,
    corpus-failures.tsv, fixture-inventory.tsv,
    fixture-inventory-summary.txt). Carries a
    compiled-in immutable SHA-256 baseline for the
    9 stage0-historical objects at 58a89cc (the
    64-hex-char digests documented in
    evidence/.../c1.2/c1.2-contract-correction.txt).
    Emits its own PASS/FAIL verdict based on the
    AC02 snapshot (166/9/175/6/0).

  tools/quality/lexer07-predecessor-verify.HC  (108 LOC)
    Direct predecessor-identity verifier (AC11b).
    Reads the closed CORRECTION04 HANDOFF, extracts
    the recorded SHA from the
    "Predecessor (closed):" line, and asserts that
    it matches the known-wrong SHA `35c67ac`
    (CORRECTION04's own C2 IMPL). On match, emits
    DEFECT_CONFIRMED and reports the correct
    predecessor `6abde99` (CORRECTION03's C4 CLOSE).
    Required because factory-closure-status-check is
    bounded to the LLVM-CORE03 universe.

  tools/quality/lexer07-mutation-stub.HC    (~5 LOC)
    AC05 mutation test: returns rc=0 with no
    evidence. The independent verifier rejects
    because its AC02 snapshot cannot match.

  tools/quality/lexer07-mutation-corrupt.HC (~30 LOC)
    AC06 mutation test: copies a stage0-historical
    .o file to /tmp, flips one byte, returns rc=0.
    The verifier rejects because the runtime SHA
    of the corrupted copy does not match the
    compiled-in baseline.

  tools/quality/lexer07-mutation-fixture.HC (~15 LOC)
    AC07 mutation test: documents the fixture-row
    removal contract (TOTAL=88 from a 89-row
    canonical).
