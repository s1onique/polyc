Why this happened (fact, not justification)
--------------------------------------------

The closure oracle's 12-pair check requires every ACT in the
manifest to have a corresponding HANDOFF file. The HANDOFF file
cannot exist before C4. The manifest self-row cannot exist before
C0 (the ACT body declares itself at C0). Therefore any closure-
oracle ACT must, at some phase between C0 and C4, modify the
manifest.

CORRECTION03's mistake was performing the modification at C0
itself rather than deferring it to C4. CORRECTION02 had done
the same thing at C0 (ca67d1a). The pattern is established but
it is NOT authorized at any ACT body so far.
